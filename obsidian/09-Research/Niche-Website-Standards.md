# Niche Website Standards: Amazon Agency Sites (2026)

**Research date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** Complete  
**Related:** [[Competitor-Analysis]], [[Design-Inspiration]], [[SEO-Architecture]]

---

## Purpose

Defines what a professional Amazon agency website must include in 2026, based on analysis of 10+ top competitors. Used as a checklist for eCom Gliders migration quality bar.

---

## Minimum viable professional site (Tier C)

Every credible Amazon agency site in 2026 includes:

### Header
- [ ] Logo linking to homepage
- [ ] Primary navigation (5-7 items max)
- [ ] Visible CTA button in header (not footer-only)
- [ ] Sticky header on scroll
- [ ] Mobile hamburger menu with full nav access

### Homepage
- [ ] Outcome-focused hero headline (not feature list)
- [ ] Primary CTA above the fold
- [ ] Trust signals (partner badges, review scores, or client logos)
- [ ] At least 3 quantified stats ($ managed, retention, team size, etc.)
- [ ] Service overview (4-6 pillars minimum)
- [ ] At least 1 case study with specific metric
- [ ] Contact path (form, phone, or both)
- [ ] FAQ section (5+ questions)

### Footer
- [ ] Service links (dedicated URLs, not anchor hashes)
- [ ] Company links (About, Contact, Blog)
- [ ] Legal links (Privacy Policy, Terms)
- [ ] Contact information (phone, email, address)
- [ ] Social media links
- [ ] Copyright notice

### Service pages
- [ ] Dedicated page per core service (not blog post template)
- [ ] Service-specific H1 and meta description
- [ ] What's included / deliverables list
- [ ] Service-specific FAQ (3-8 questions)
- [ ] CTA to free audit or contact

### Technical
- [ ] Mobile responsive
- [ ] Page load under 3 seconds (target)
- [ ] HTTPS
- [ ] Structured data (Organization, Service or Article, FAQPage where applicable)
- [ ] Accessible contrast (WCAG AA minimum)

## Competitive professional site (Tier B)

What separates boutique premium agencies (Olifant, top mid-market) from basic sites:

### Header (enhanced)
- [ ] Top announcement bar (review score, key metric, or offer)
- [ ] Mega menu with service descriptions
- [ ] Inline CTA panel inside mega menu dropdown
- [ ] Single nav system (no duplicates)

### Homepage (enhanced)
- [ ] 4 stat cards above or immediately below fold
- [ ] 3+ case studies with metric + quote + read more
- [ ] Seller segmentation ("Who we serve" by stage or category)
- [ ] 4-step process visualization
- [ ] 6-8 named testimonials with role and company
- [ ] 10-12 FAQs with FAQPage schema
- [ ] Blog/insights section linking to content library
- [ ] Agency vs DIY comparison table (AEO value)
- [ ] Final CTA band with form + phone

### Case studies
- [ ] Dedicated case study index page
- [ ] Filterable by service type
- [ ] Individual case study detail pages
- [ ] Before/after metrics (minimum 3 per case)
- [ ] Named client quote with role

### Trust and credibility
- [ ] Amazon partner or verified badge (if applicable)
- [ ] Review platform score (Clutch, Trustpilot, Google)
- [ ] Guarantee or risk reversal (if policy exists)
- [ ] Named team or "in-house specialists" statement
- [ ] Honest metric presentation (no inflation)

### SEO and content
- [ ] Primary keyword in homepage title and H1
- [ ] Service pages optimized for service-specific keywords
- [ ] Blog with regular publishing (eCom Gliders: 328 posts)
- [ ] Internal linking between services, cases, and blog
- [ ] Canonical URLs on all indexable pages

## Enterprise premium site (Tier A)

What enterprise agencies (Canopy, Sequence, MAG) add. eCom Gliders does not need all of these:

### Scale signals
- [ ] $1B+ or $500M+ managed revenue stated
- [ ] 100+ brands or 100+ team members
- [ ] Multi-office global presence
- [ ] 10+ industry verticals
- [ ] 280+ review scores

### Premium features
- [ ] Video case studies with founder quotes
- [ ] Awards wall (10+ verified badges)
- [ ] Proprietary tools or platforms
- [ ] Application-based onboarding flow
- [ ] Multi-channel service pages (Amazon, Walmart, Google, etc.)

**eCom Gliders note:** Do not claim Tier A scale. Compete on retention, personal service, and content depth instead.

## 2026 emerging standards

New patterns appearing in leading agencies:

### Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)
- [ ] Direct answer sections (concise definitions)
- [ ] FAQ with structured data
- [ ] Comparison tables (agency vs alternatives)
- [ ] Entity-consistent company information across pages

### AI SEO / GEO
- [ ] Clear service capability statements (machine-readable)
- [ ] Consistent facts (founding year, team size, metrics)
- [ ] Authoritative blog content (eCom Gliders strength: 328 posts)
- [ ] Schema markup on all key pages

Sequence Commerce leads here with dedicated AIO/GEO sections. eCom Gliders should adopt FAQ and comparison patterns without fake AI recommendation blocks.

### Conversion optimization
- [ ] Sticky header CTA
- [ ] Dedicated free audit landing page (not just footer form)
- [ ] Phone number visible in header or hero
- [ ] Short forms (3-5 fields maximum)
- [ ] WhatsApp or chat as supplementary (not primary)

## Content quality standards

### Copy
- Professional Amazon agency voice
- Specific outcomes, not vague claims
- Credible metrics only (verified before reuse)
- No emoji, no Unicode symbols as icons
- No AI marketing clichés ("magic", "super powerful", "AI-powered" without proof)

### Images
- Real client work or brand assets where possible
- No generic stock photography for case studies
- Alt text on all images
- Optimized file sizes (WebP preferred)

### Forms
- Server-side validation
- CSRF protection on state-changing requests
- No logging of sensitive form data
- Clear privacy statement near form
