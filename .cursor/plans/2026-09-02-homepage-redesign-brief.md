# Homepage Redesign Brief

**Date:** 2026-09-02  
**Status:** Proposed (pending approval before implementation)  
**Related:** [[Design-Inspiration]], [[Page-Templates]], [[Navigation-Footer-Spec]], [[Design-System]]

---

## Objective

Redesign eCom Gliders homepage to Tier B boutique premium (Olifant Digital) with Tier C conversion patterns (AMZ Dudes), while preserving brand identity and honest metrics.

---

## Positioning

**Headline direction:** Full-service Amazon growth for ambitious FBA brands.

**Differentiation:** "Full-service Amazon account management with an in-house team of specialists. Not a call center. Not a software dashboard. Real people who treat your brand like their own."

**Target audience:** Mid-market Amazon sellers (scaling brands, multi-ASIN catalogs, new sellers ready to invest).

## 12-section wireframe

### Section 1: Hero

**Layout:** Split (Canopy/MAG enterprise + Cursor product calm). Text left, one BrowserMockup right with metric overlays.

| Element | Content |
|---------|---------|
| Eyebrow | Amazon Ads Verified Partner |
| H1 | Full-service Amazon growth for ambitious FBA brands (`text-hero` 40–56px clamp) |
| Subheadline | PPC, listing optimization, content, and catalog management from an in-house team of Amazon specialists. |
| Primary CTA | Get Free Audit (accent) to `/free-audit-2/` |
| Secondary CTA | View case studies to `/case-studies/` |
| Support line | In-house specialists. Not a call center. Not a software dashboard. |
| Trust | Partner badge image + Helping sellers since 2018 |
| Visual | Desktop macOS / mobile iOS BrowserMockup + verified metric overlays ($112M+, 95%) |

**Reference:** Canopy dual CTA + proof overlays; MAG metric-on-device; Cursor single product frame. Audit: `docs/research/HERO-COMPETITOR-UI-AUDIT-2026-09-03.md`.

**Avoid:** Form-in-hero, dark cinematic, dual mockup stack, unverified claims.

## What to remove from current homepage

- Duplicate navigation systems (WPR mega + Elementor nav)
- "Home" in primary nav (logo handles this)
- Testimonials as separate nav item (becomes section 9)
- Anchor-based service links (#service_2)
- Hidden "Inactive" headings in mega menu
