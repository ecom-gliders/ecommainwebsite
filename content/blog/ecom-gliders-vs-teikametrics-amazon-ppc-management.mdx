## How do eCom Gliders and Teikametrics compare for Amazon PPC?

eCom Gliders is a full-service Amazon seller growth agency that manages PPC alongside listings, creative, catalog, and account work. Teikametrics publicly positions as an AI marketplace optimization platform (ads, catalog, inventory, and insights) with self-serve software tiers and optional managed services (Observed on teikametrics.com, 2026-10-30). The useful comparison is agency execution versus software-first retail intelligence—not a claim that one model always wins on ACOS.

Competitor product names, pricing, and managed-service scope change. Verify Teikametrics' current plans directly before buying. ## What Teikametrics publicly offers

Based on Teikametrics public platform, pricing, and managed-services pages (Observed 2026-10-30), Teikametrics markets:

*   AI-powered advertising optimization for marketplace sellers
*   Software suites spanning ads, catalog, inventory, and insights under its ARI product messaging
*   Self-serve and higher-tier plans, with public pricing examples for lower spend bands and custom pricing for larger accounts
*   Optional managed services where strategists work inside the Teikametrics platform
*   Multi-marketplace advertising support messaging (Amazon, Walmart, TikTok Shop)

That is a software-centered model. Managed services may feel agency-like, but the product identity remains platform-led. Confirm what is included in any managed package versus self-serve software alone.

## Is Teikametrics fully automated?

Not completely, in the sense of "no humans involved." Public Teikametrics materials describe AI optimization software and also offer managed services with strategists (Observed 2026-10-30). Automation level depends on whether you buy self-serve software, managed support, or both.

## Agency vs software: how to choose

**Consider eCom Gliders if you:**

*   Need specialists to rebuild campaign structure and improve listings together
*   Want creative, A+ Content, and catalog work under one Amazon agency
*   Prefer audit-led prioritization before raising spend
*   Do not want your growth plan limited to software recommendations alone

**Consider evaluating Teikametrics if you:**

*   Want an AI software workflow for ads and related marketplace data
*   Have internal operators who can act on platform recommendations
*   Are comparing software tiers or Teikametrics managed services against agency retainers
*   Need multi-marketplace tooling in one vendor conversation

Some brands combine software and agencies. The mismatch to avoid is automating bids into a listing shoppers will not convert on—or hiring an agency when you only needed reporting software.

## Pricing note

Teikametrics publishes software pricing bands on its public pricing page for some tiers; managed and enterprise plans are often custom. Agency retainers are typically scoped after audit. Compare written scopes, not list-price assumptions.

## Bottom line

Teikametrics is best evaluated as AI retail software with optional managed support. eCom Gliders is best evaluated as a full-service Amazon agency where PPC is one lever among listings, creative, and catalog health. Match the model to the bottleneck.
