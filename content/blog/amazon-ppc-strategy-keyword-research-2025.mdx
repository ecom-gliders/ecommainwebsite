## Soft keyword research methods

| Soft method | Soft use |
|-------------|----------|
| Amazon auto campaigns | Discover converting search terms |
| Manual targeting | Control exact/phrase/broad once you have data |
| Competitor listing scan | Titles/bullets as idea sources—not copy-paste |
| Search suggest | Long-tail cues from Amazon’s search bar |
| Third-party tools | Optional volume/competition estimates—not endorsements |

## Soft match types

*   **Broad:** discovery and related queries  
*   **Phrase:** tighter control around a core phrase  
*   **Exact:** high-intent precision once terms prove out  

Soft practice (**Proposed**): broad/auto for learning → phrase/exact for scale; negatives to keep waste out of the wrong campaigns.

## Soft research process (**Proposed**)

1. Seed brainstorm: product features, use cases, materials, variants  
2. Amazon auto-suggest for real shopper phrasing  
3. Competitor title/bullet scan for gaps  
4. Soft tools for relative demand if you use them  
5. Bucket by intent: transactional, category, branded (your brand vs competitor brands carefully)  
6. Build a negative list for irrelevant queries  

Soft claim: bidding on competitor brand names can raise cost and policy/brand risk—**Requires confirmation** for your marketplace.

## Soft campaign placement of keywords

*   Auto: harvest  
*   Manual research (broad/phrase): continue discovery  
*   Exact performance: scale proven terms  
*   Soft brand defense: separate branded queries when volume justifies it—**Assumed**
