## How to fix high ACoS on Amazon PPC

Soft short answer: high ACoS usually means **wasted search terms, weak conversion, or bids above what your margin can support**—fix those before “scaling.” Soft claim: there is no universal “good ACoS”; it depends on contribution margin and goals—**Assumed**. ## Soft ACoS vs TACOS

| Metric | Soft meaning |
|--------|----------------|
| ACoS | Ad spend ÷ ad-attributed sales |
| TACOS | Ad spend ÷ total sales (paid + organic) |

Soft claim: low ACoS with rising TACOS can still be unhealthy if organic is flat—**Assumed**. Soft claim: break-even ACoS sits near your contribution margin after COGS, fees, and returns—**Assumed** math; calculate yours.

## Soft triage when ACoS is high

1. **Margin check** — Is the target ACoS realistic for this ASIN?  
2. **Conversion check** — CTR and unit session % vs peers; fix listing before more spend  
3. **Search-term hygiene** — Negate irrelevant queries; harvest converters into exact/phrase  
4. **Structure** — Separate brand / non-brand / competitor; avoid dumping all keywords into one ad group  
5. **Bids & placements** — Reduce bids on wasteful placements; raise only where conversion proves out  
6. **Inventory** — Pause or throttle ads on OOS / low-stock ASINs  

## Soft campaign hygiene (**Proposed**)

*   Weekly search-term harvest and negatives  
*   Match-type discipline (broad for discovery, exact for efficiency)  
*   Portfolio budgets that starve losers, not winners  
*   Soft claim: automation helps; it does not replace unit economics—**Assumed**  

## Soft before you “scale”

Soft claim: scale spend only after ACoS (or TACOS) is inside an agreed band for 1–2 review cycles. Soft practice: increase budgets gradually and watch conversion and stock. Soft claim: DSP / international expansion are optional layers—not the first fix for a broken Sponsored Products account—**Assumed**.

## Bottom line

Soft claim: fixing high ACoS is structured triage—margin, conversion, terms, structure, bids—not AI hype. Soft claim: results vary by category competition and listing quality.
