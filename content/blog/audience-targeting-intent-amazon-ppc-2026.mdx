## Audience targeting and intent in Amazon PPC (beyond keywords)

Soft claim: Amazon PPC has moved beyond keyword matching alone toward behavioral signals, automation, and audience layers. Soft claim: keywords remain useful; they are not the whole strategy. Soft claim: outcomes depend on competition, creative, listing quality, and budget—not calendar year alone—**Assumed**.

## Why keyword-only models lose leverage

Soft claim: high-intent terms often get crowded; CPC pressure rises—**Assumed**. Soft claim: Amazon also expands automated targeting and machine-assisted delivery, so advertiser control at the keyword level can shrink. Soft direction: think in shoppers and stages, not only search terms.

## Audience targeting signals (soft)

Audience targeting reaches shoppers by behavior and history, not only typed queries. Soft signal examples (**Assumed**; availability varies):

*   Purchase history (repeat, loyalist, switcher)  
*   Category browsing and affinity  
*   Deal/price responsiveness  
*   Seasonal and time-based patterns  
*   Brand affinity / competitive interest  

Soft formats where audience layers often appear: Sponsored Display, Sponsored Brands video, Amazon DSP—**Requires confirmation** for your region.

## Intent as a practical lens

Soft claim: two shoppers can share a keyword with different readiness. Soft intent cues (**Assumed**): PDP time, comparisons, cart adds/removes, coupon checks, short-window revisits, adjacent-category purchases. Soft practice: align bid aggressiveness, creative, and offers to discovery / consideration / conversion-ready stages instead of treating every click equally.

## Soft campaign architecture by audience

**Proposed** layers (not a guarantee of efficiency):

*   Purchase-ready / high intent  
*   Competitor-consideration  
*   Repeat / loyalty  
*   New-to-brand discovery  
*   Seasonal or event-driven  

Soft creative mapping: conversion proof and offers for high intent; education and brand story for discovery. Soft measurement: ROAS alone can miss incrementality—track new-to-brand, overlap, and cross-campaign influence where available—**Assumed**.

## DSP and PPC as one funnel (soft)

Soft closed loop (**Proposed**): DSP for awareness → Sponsored Brands video for reinforcement → Sponsored Products for high-intent search → Sponsored Display for non-converters. Soft claim: consistent messaging reduces wasted spend; it does not guarantee ROAS.

## How sellers can prepare

1. Strengthen listing quality and CX (signals feed delivery)  
2. Test audience-capable formats early with clear hypotheses  
3. Build creative variants by intent stage  
4. Measure incrementality where tools allow—not only last-click ROAS  
5. Keep keyword hygiene where it still informs search coverage  

## Bottom line

Use audiences and intent to complement keywords. Soft claim: that improves targeting discipline; it does not replace product-market fit or listing conversion.
