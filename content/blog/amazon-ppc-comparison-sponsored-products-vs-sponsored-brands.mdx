## Sponsored Products vs Sponsored Brands: soft comparison

Soft short answer: **Sponsored Products** push single ASINs toward conversion in search and PDPs; **Sponsored Brands** push brand presence (logo, headline, multi-ASIN or storefront) for awareness and catalog storytelling. ## Soft Sponsored Products

*   Single-listing ads in search and product pages  
*   Keyword, product, or auto targeting  
*   Soft fit: launches, conversion tests, keyword competition—**Assumed**  
*   Soft claim: “highest conversion” vs Brands is category-dependent—**Requires confirmation**

## Soft Sponsored Brands

*   Brand assets, headline, multi-product or store creatives  
*   Soft fit: Brand Registry sellers building recognition and catalog cross-sell—**Assumed**  
*   Soft claim: Brand Registry and creative policy eligibility **Requires confirmation**

| Soft dimension | Soft Sponsored Products | Soft Sponsored Brands |
|----------------|-------------------------|------------------------|

| Soft creative | Mostly listing-driven | Logo, headline, multi-ASIN |
