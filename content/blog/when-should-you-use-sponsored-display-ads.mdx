## Learn when Sponsored Display Ads can maximize your Amazon advertising results.

### Introduction
Amazon advertising has become increasingly competitive, making it essential for sellers to use more than just Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands campaigns. One advertising format that continues to gain importance is **Sponsored Display Ads**. These campaigns allow sellers to reach shoppers both on and off Amazon using audience signals, shopping behavior, and product targeting.

However, many sellers launch Sponsored Display campaigns without understanding when they provide the greatest value. As a result, budgets are often spent inefficiently with limited returns. Knowing the right situations to use **[Sponsored Display Ads](http://www.sellersprite.com/en/blog/Amazon-Sponsored-Display-Ads-Complete-Guide-Targeting-Setup-Placements-and-Best-Practices)** can help maximize visibility, improve conversions, and increase long-term profitability.

This guide explains when Sponsored Display Ads should be part of your Amazon advertising strategy and how they can support business growth in 2026.

![When Should You Use Sponsored Display Ads? | Amazon PPC Strategy Guide 2026](/images/blog/ChatGPT-Image-Jul-20-2026-07_06_33-AM-1024x576-11c8bf5c.png)

### What Are Sponsored Display Ads?

Sponsored Display Ads are a self-service Amazon advertising solution that enables brands and sellers to reach shoppers throughout different stages of the buying journey. Unlike Sponsored Products, which mainly target keywords within Amazon search results, Sponsored Display campaigns focus on [**audience targeting and product targeting.**](https://www.adverio.io/dtc-amazon-integration-2/)

These ads can appear:

*   Amazon product detail pages
*   Customer review pages
*   Amazon homepage
*   Mobile app placements
*   Third-party websites and apps (where eligible)

The primary goal is to reconnect with interested shoppers while expanding visibility beyond traditional search results.

#### When Should You Use Sponsored Display Ads?

    **1. Retarget Shoppers Who Viewed Your Products:**

One of the strongest use cases for Sponsored Display Ads is retargeting.

Many shoppers browse products but leave Amazon without purchasing. Sponsored Display campaigns help remind these potential customers about products they previously viewed, increasing the chances of conversion.

This works particularly well for:

*   High-value products
*   Products requiring research
*   Seasonal purchases
*   Competitive categories

Retargeting keeps products visible even after shoppers leave the original product page.

**2\. Protect Product Detail Pages from Competitors:**

Competitors constantly advertise on each other’s listings.

Sponsored Display Ads allow sellers to defend their own product pages by placing complementary or related products on their listings instead of allowing competitors to dominate customer attention.

Benefits include:

*   Increased brand visibility
*   Higher cross-selling opportunities
*   Better customer retention
*   Improved conversion rates

**3\. Launching a New Product:**

New product launches often struggle because they have:

*   Limited reviews
*   Little sales history
*   Minimal organic ranking

Sponsored Display campaigns help generate early visibility while Sponsored Products focus on keyword traffic.

A combined strategy accelerates:

*   Initial impressions
*   Product awareness
*   Click-through rate
*   Sales velocity

This additional traffic can support stronger organic performance over time.

**4\. Increase Brand Awareness:**

Not every advertising campaign should focus only on immediate sales.

Sponsored Display Ads are excellent for introducing products to shoppers who may not yet know the brand.

They help businesses:

*   Reach relevant audiences
*   Build familiarity
*   Increase product exposure
*   Support long-term customer acquisition

For growing brands, awareness campaigns often create stronger performance across future advertising efforts.

**5\. Cross-Sell Your Product Catalog:**

Sponsored Display campaigns can recommend related products from the same brand.

For example:

If a customer purchases:

*   Protein Powder

They can later see ads for:

*   Shaker Bottles
*   Vitamins
*   Gym Accessories

Cross-selling increases average order value while strengthening customer loyalty.

 

When Should You Use Sponsored Display Ads?

The four most effective situations where Sponsored Display Ads deliver the highest ROI.

```feature-grid
Retarget Visitor | Reconnect with shoppers who viewed your products but didn’t purchase.
Launch Product | Generate awareness and accelerate sales for new product launches.
Protect Listings | Keep competitors from stealing customers on your product pages.
Scale Sales | Increase conversions with audience and product targeting.
```

### Benefits of Sponsored Display Ads:

Businesses using Sponsored Display campaigns strategically can experience several advantages.

These include:

*   Better audience targeting
*   Increased brand visibility
*   Higher conversion opportunities
*   Improved remarketing performance
*   Cross-selling opportunities
*   Product page protection
*   Enhanced campaign diversification
*   Greater customer lifetime value

When combined with Sponsored Products and Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display campaigns create a more complete advertising strategy.

#### Best Practices for Sponsored Display Ads:

To maximize advertising performance, sellers should follow several best practices.

#### Use Product Targeting Carefully:

Choose competitor products that closely match your own pricing, quality, and customer expectations.

#### Segment Campaigns:

Separate campaigns by:

*   Product category
*   Audience type
*   Budget
*   Goals

This improves optimization and reporting.

#### Monitor Performance Metrics:

Regularly review:

*   [**Click-Through Rate (CTR)**](https://ecomgliders.com/services/#service_5)
*   Advertising Cost of Sales (ACoS)
*   Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
*   Impressions
*   Conversion Rate
*   Orders

Performance data helps identify campaigns that need adjustment.

#### Refresh Creative Assets:

Keep product images, pricing, and messaging updated to remain competitive and relevant.

#### Combine with Other Amazon Ad Types:

Sponsored Display performs best when integrated with:

*   Sponsored Products
*   Sponsored Brands
*   Brand Store
*   Organic SEO
*   High-quality product listings

A balanced advertising strategy often produces stronger long-term results than relying on a single campaign type.

#### Common Mistakes to Avoid:

Many sellers fail to achieve positive results because they make avoidable mistakes.

Common issues include:

*   Launching campaigns without clear objectives
*   Targeting overly broad audiences
*   Ignoring campaign optimization
*   Setting unrealistic budgets
*   Failing to monitor ACoS
*   Using weak product listings
*   Running campaigns without enough historical data

Regular optimization is essential for maintaining advertising efficiency.

#### Final Thoughts:

Sponsored Display Ads are most effective when used with a clear objective rather than as a replacement for other Amazon advertising formats. Whether the goal is to retarget shoppers, protect product pages, launch new products, increase brand awareness, or encourage repeat purchases, these campaigns can play an important role in a well-rounded Amazon PPC strategy.

As Amazon advertising continues to evolve in 2026, sellers who combine Sponsored Display Ads with optimized listings, strong keyword research, and continuous performance analysis are better positioned to reach qualified audiences and drive sustainable growth. By focusing on the right timing and targeting methods, businesses can make their advertising budgets work more efficiently while building long-term success on the marketplace.
