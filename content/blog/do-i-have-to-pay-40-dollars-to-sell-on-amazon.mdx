## Do I have to pay $40 to sell on Amazon?

Soft short answer: **No—you can sell on Amazon without a ~$40 monthly subscription** by using the Individual selling plan (per-item fee instead). Soft claim: most brands that scale still choose Professional for ads, reports, and tooling—**Assumed**. Soft claim: exact dollar amounts change by marketplace and date—**Requires confirmation** on Amazon’s current Selling on Amazon fee page. Related: [Amazon PPC](/amazon-ppc/) and [free Amazon audit](/free-audit/).

![Amazon Individual vs Professional selling plan fee choice.](/images/blog/ChatGPT-Image-Nov-21-2025-02_09_27-AM-a30e954b.png)

## Soft plan comparison (US framing)

| Plan | Soft typical structure (**Requires confirmation**) | Soft fit |
|------|-----------------------------------------------------|----------|
| Individual | $0 monthly + per-item fee (often cited near $0.99/item) | Testing, very low volume |
| Professional | Flat monthly fee (often cited near $39.99) + no per-item selling fee | Scaling, brands, PPC |

Soft claim: referral fees, FBA fees, storage, and ads sit on top of either plan.

## Soft when Individual is enough

*   Occasional or clearance selling  
*   Very low monthly unit volume  
*   You are validating demand before committing capital  
*   You do not need Sponsored ads yet  

## Soft when Professional is usually better

*   You need [Amazon PPC](/amazon-ppc/) / Sponsored Products  
*   You want fuller Business Reports and bulk tools  
*   You are building a private-label brand (Brand Registry workflows commonly assume Professional)—**Assumed**  
*   Your per-item fees would exceed the monthly subscription at your volume  

Soft teaching example: if the per-item fee is ~$0.99, about **40 units/month** approaches a ~$40 flat fee—**Assumed** arithmetic; verify live fee cards.

## Soft costs beyond the subscription

*   Referral fees by category  
*   FBA fulfillment and storage (if used)  
*   Inbound freight and prep  
*   Advertising budgets  
*   Returns and COGS  

Soft claim: the subscription is rarely the largest line item for a serious catalog—**Assumed**.

## Bottom line

Soft claim: paying ~$40/month is **not mandatory** to list and sell, but it is often the practical path once you advertise and scale. Soft practice: confirm current fees in Seller Central for your marketplace before budgeting.
