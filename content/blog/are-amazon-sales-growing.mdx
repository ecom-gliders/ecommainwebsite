## Are Amazon sales growing?

Soft short answer: **Amazon’s reported company revenue has generally expanded over multi-year horizons across diversified segments**—but exact YoY rates change by quarter and must be read from Amazon’s latest filings—**Requires confirmation**. Soft claim: “Amazon is growing” is not the same as “my listing is growing.” Soft twins: seller-level trend → [are Amazon sales increasing](/are-amazon-sales-increasing/); drop diagnosis → [decline in Amazon sales](/decline-in-amazon-sales/); boycott window → [Amazon sales after boycott](/amazon-sales-after-boycott-analysis/). ## Soft what “Amazon sales” can mean

| Lens | Soft meaning |
|------|----------------|
| Company net sales | Amazon.com, Inc. consolidated revenue |
| Online stores / marketplace | 1P + 3P retail GMV proxies in disclosures |
| AWS | Cloud segment revenue |
| Advertising | Retail media / ads line |
| Seller account | Your sessions, units, ordered product sales |

Soft claim: mixing these lenses causes false conclusions—**Assumed**.

## Soft company-level growth drivers (not a forecast)

Public Amazon reporting and common analyst framing emphasize (**Assumed** / **Requires confirmation** against the current 10-K/10-Q):

*   Third-party marketplace scale and FBA network effects  
*   Prime habit and purchase frequency  
*   AWS enterprise demand  
*   Advertising / retail media  
*   International expansion (uneven by country)  

Soft claim: retail can look “slow” while ads or AWS still support overall growth narratives.

## Soft why your sales can fall while Amazon grows

*   Category saturation or seasonality  
*   Stockouts, Buy Box loss, or suppressed listings  
*   Ad efficiency decline (ACoS/TACOS)  
*   Competition and price pressure  
*   Content / conversion gaps  

Soft practice: compare your YoY weeks to category peers before blaming “the platform stopped growing.”

## Soft seller checklist

1. Ordered product sales and sessions (YoY and prior 4–8 weeks)  
2. Organic vs paid share  
3. Conversion and Buy Box %  
4. Inventory health  
5. Peer ASINs in the same subcategory  

## Bottom line

Soft claim: Amazon as a company often shows multi-segment growth in disclosed results, but that does not guarantee growth for every seller. Soft claim: verify the latest Amazon financial disclosures for company figures; diagnose your account for seller figures.
