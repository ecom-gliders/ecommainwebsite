## Why is Amazon PPC draining your advertising budget?

Amazon PPC budgets drain when campaigns target broad keywords, skip negative keywords, and leave bids unmanaged. Review Search Term Reports weekly, cut waste, and optimize for conversions, ACOS, and ROAS before you raise spend.

Amazon PPC should grow sales, not only consume budget. Many sellers see ad spend rise while profits stay flat. That usually comes from structure and hygiene gaps, not from advertising itself.

For managed campaign structure, see [Amazon PPC management](/amazon-ppc-management-agency/).

### Why your Amazon PPC budget disappears so fast

Common causes include targeting broad keywords, ignoring negative keywords, and failing to optimize bids regularly. Those issues raise cost while delivering weak results.

### PPC mistakes that waste spend

**Ignoring Search Term Reports:** Search term reports show where budget is spent. Without reviewing them, you may keep paying for irrelevant clicks.

**Not using negative keywords:** Negatives block unqualified traffic and reduce wasted ad spend.

**Poor bid management:** Setting bids once and forgetting them can cause overspend or missed opportunities.

**Focusing only on clicks:** Clicks do not matter if they do not convert. Track conversions, ACOS, and profitability.

### How to fix your Amazon PPC strategy

*   Audit campaigns regularly
*   Add negative keywords weekly
*   Optimize bids based on performance
*   Focus on high-converting search terms
*   Monitor ACOS and ROAS consistently

### What to do next

Amazon PPC remains a strong growth channel when it is optimized continuously, not when spend is increased without diagnosis. Fix common PPC mistakes and use performance data to reduce waste and protect profit.
