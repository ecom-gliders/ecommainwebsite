## Amazon PPC Is Draining Your Wallet Here's Why

## Amazon PPC Optimization Strategies That Work in 2026

Running [Amazon PPC campaigns](https://ecomgliders.com/services/#service_5) should help you grow sales not drain your budget. Yet many sellers watch their ad spend rise while profits stay flat. If that sounds familiar, you’re not alone.

### Why Your Amazon PPC Budget Disappears So Fast:

Many sellers make the mistake of targeting broad keywords, ignoring negative keywords, and failing to optimize bids regularly. These issues can quickly increase costs while delivering poor results.

### The Hidden PPC Mistakes Costing You Sales:

**Ignoring Search Term Reports:**

Search term reports reveal exactly where your budget is being spent. Without reviewing them, you may continue paying for irrelevant clicks.

**Not Using Negative Keywords:**

Negative keywords help block unqualified traffic and reduce wasted ad spend.

**Poor Bid Management:**

Setting bids once and forgetting them can lead to overspending or missed opportunities.

**Focusing Only on Clicks:**

Clicks don’t matter if they don’t convert. Always track conversions, ACoS, and profitability.

### How to Fix Your Amazon PPC Strategy:

*   Audit campaigns regularly
*   Add negative keywords weekly
*   Optimize bids based on performance
*   Focus on high-converting search terms
*   Monitor ACoS and ROAS consistently

### The Bottom Line:

[Amazon PPC](https://www.sellerqi.com/blog/amazon-ppc-feels-expensive-2026) remains one of the most powerful growth tools for sellers in 2026. However, success comes from continuous optimization not simply increasing ad spend. By fixing common PPC mistakes and focusing on data-driven decisions, you can reduce wasted budget and achieve better profitability.
