## A step-by-step method to validate products on Amazon

Validate before you buy inventory. Soft claim: a multi-step check reduces unforced errors; it does not guarantee sales. Use this ordered method. For selection framing see [choose winning products without guesswork](/choose-winning-products-without-guesswork/); for saturation risks see [avoid saturated niche price wars](/avoid-saturated-niche-price-wars-amazon/); for demand/competition reading see [smart way to research demand and competition](/smart-way-to-research-demand-and-competition-on-amazon/).

![Step-by-step Amazon product validation.](/images/blog/ChatGPT-Image-Nov-22-2025-08_25_09-PM-1024x683-8af011da.png)

## Step 1: Confirm demand with multiple signals

Do not rely on one metric. Soft checks:

*   Search volume trends for primary and secondary keywords
*   Best Seller Rank stability over roughly 90–180 days when history exists
*   Estimated monthly sales across several top listings
*   Demand depth beyond one or two ASINs
*   Review timelines that hint at ongoing purchases

Soft pass criteria: steadier sales, commercial-intent keywords, and revenue without constant heavy discounting—**Requires confirmation** with your data.

## Step 2: Score competition by listing strength

Seller count alone is weak. Review creative quality, review velocity, brand vs private-label mix, price clusters, and Sponsored density. Soft claim: weak listings in a crowded niche can be an opening; strong brands in a thin niche can still block you. Soft viability: you need a realistic path to better value or positioning—not a promise you will outperform.

## Step 3: Model profitability at competitive ad spend

Include landed cost, FBA/referral/storage fees, and advertising projections. Soft heuristic (**Requires confirmation**): many sellers stress-test contribution after ads rather than locking a fixed 25–35% net. If margins break under realistic ACOS, fail the product even if demand looks fine.

## Step 4: Prove a customer-visible difference

Mine 1–3 star reviews for repeated complaints, feature gaps, bundle ideas, branding gaps, and compliance notes buyers care about. Soft claim: a clear, deliverable improvement helps; “instantly recognizable” is not automatic.

## Step 5: Confirm supply chain feasibility

Check alternate suppliers, MOQ flexibility, lead times vs inventory cycles, packaging/compliance readiness, freight reliability, and currency/geopolitical exposure. Soft claim: fragile logistics raise stockout and cash-flow risk. Official prep context: [Prepare to sell on Amazon](https://sell.amazon.com/learn/prepare-to-sell).

## Bottom line

Demand, competition, economics, differentiation, and supply must all pass. Soft outcome: fewer bad POs—not guaranteed brand scale.
