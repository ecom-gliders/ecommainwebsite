## Soft pressures that raise cancel reviews

*   **Price increases** force a “do I still use this?” audit  
*   **Prime Video ads** (ad-free as paid upsell in some markets)—value shock for entertainment-led members—**Assumed**  
*   **Subscription fatigue** across streaming and shopping memberships  
*   **Retail alternatives** (Walmart+, Target/Shipt, other marketplaces) reduce “Amazon by default”  
*   **Benefit downgrades** (e.g., music tier changes) that feel like paying more for less—**Assumed**  

## Soft retention strengths

*   Shipping habit and delivery network depth in many regions—**Assumed**  
*   Bundle value across shopping + video + grocery where available  
*   Device and ecosystem lock-in (Fire TV, Alexa, Kindle)—**Assumed**  
*   Ongoing exclusive deals and logistics investment  

Soft claim: retention can hold even while churn conversations rise online.
