## How do you lower Amazon ACOS without stopping PPC?

Lower Amazon ACOS by improving keyword quality, campaign structure, Search Term Report hygiene, and listing conversion before you raise budget. Spend smarter on profitable terms; do not only spend more.

Running Amazon PPC campaigns can feel like a tradeoff between visibility and cost. Rising advertising spend can reduce profit if campaigns lack structure. Spending more money on ads does not automatically create more profitable sales.

Successful sellers spend smarter. If Advertising Cost of Sales (ACOS) keeps rising while profits shrink, optimize with keyword quality, campaign structure, and data-driven decisions rather than pausing all ads.

This guide covers the Amazon PPC process professional sellers use to reduce wasted ad spend, improve conversions, and strengthen return on investment. For managed execution, see [Amazon PPC management](/amazon-ppc-management-agency/).

### What is Amazon PPC?

Amazon PPC (Pay-Per-Click) is Amazon's advertising platform that lets sellers promote products in search results and on product pages. You pay when a shopper clicks your ad.

Amazon currently offers three primary advertising options:

*   **Sponsored Products** for promoting individual listings.
*   **Sponsored Brands** to showcase your brand and multiple products.
*   **Sponsored Display** for retargeting shoppers and reaching audiences on and off Amazon.

For most beginners, Sponsored Products deliver the highest return because they target shoppers already searching for similar products.

### Why Amazon PPC matters

A well-managed PPC campaign helps you:

*   Increase product visibility.
*   Generate more sales.
*   Improve organic keyword rankings.
*   Launch new products faster.
*   Collect valuable customer search data.

Without optimization, PPC campaigns can become expensive quickly.

### Understanding ACOS

Before improving campaigns, understand Advertising Cost of Sales (ACOS).

**ACOS = (Advertising Spend / Advertising Sales) x 100**

| Advertising spend | Sales generated | ACOS |
| --- | --- | --- |
| $150 | $750 | 20% |

That means you spent 20 cents on advertising for every dollar earned through PPC sales.

### Is a lower ACOS always better?

Not necessarily.

A campaign with a 15% ACOS may generate very few sales, while another with a 28% ACOS could produce higher profit through greater volume.

Instead of asking only "How can I lower my ACOS?", ask:

*   Is my campaign profitable?
*   Is advertising increasing total sales?
*   Am I improving organic rankings?
*   Is my conversion rate healthy?

The objective is not only lower ACOS. It is maximum profitable contribution.

### Master the Search Term Report

Your Search Term Report is one of the most valuable tools in Amazon Advertising. It shows the exact words customers typed before clicking your ad.

Many beginners confuse keywords with search terms.

*   **Keyword:** The phrase you add to your campaign.
*   **Search Term:** The actual phrase a customer uses on Amazon.

This report helps you discover profitable long-tail keywords and identify wasteful searches.

### Review your Search Term Report every week

*   Add high-converting search terms to Exact Match campaigns.
*   Reduce bids for expensive keywords.
*   Add irrelevant terms as negative keywords.
*   Pause consistently unprofitable keywords.

Small weekly improvements often produce better long-term results than large one-time changes.

### Improve your listing before raising budget

Many sellers increase PPC budget when sales slow down. The problem often sits in the product listing, not only the ads.

Before spending more, ask:

*   Are your images high quality?
*   Does your main image stand out?
*   Is your title optimized with relevant keywords?
*   Do your bullet points highlight benefits?
*   Is your pricing competitive?
*   Do you have enough positive reviews?

A stronger listing improves conversion rate, so more visitors become buyers. When conversions rise, ad efficiency often improves and ACOS can fall. Pair this with [Amazon listing optimization](/amazon-listing-optimization/).

### Weekly Amazon PPC optimization checklist

Review campaigns consistently, not only once a month.

*   Download the Search Term Report.
*   Add profitable search terms to Manual Exact campaigns.
*   Add irrelevant searches as negative keywords.
*   Lower bids on expensive keywords with poor conversions.
*   Increase bids slightly on profitable keywords.
*   Check click-through rate (CTR).
*   Monitor conversion rate (CVR).
*   Review ACOS and ROAS.
*   Pause keywords that continue to waste budget.
*   Analyze campaign performance before major changes.

A disciplined optimization routine often outperforms impulsive daily changes.

### Common Amazon PPC mistakes

**Using only Broad Match:** Broad Match is useful for research but should not be the long-term strategy for every keyword.

**Ignoring negative keywords:** Irrelevant clicks cost money. Add negatives regularly.

**Increasing bids too quickly:** Wait for enough data before raising bids.

**Optimizing every day:** Amazon's algorithm needs learning time. Constant changes can reset learning and mislead decisions.

**Advertising weak listings:** Ads cannot consistently overcome weak images, unclear copy, or uncompetitive pricing.

### ACOS reduction as a process

The most reliable way to lower ACOS is not a secret button or expensive software. It is a disciplined optimization process built around data.

Separate keyword research from high-performing campaigns, review Search Term Reports regularly, use negative keywords, optimize bids, and improve the product listing. That combination reduces wasted spend while protecting sales growth.

Amazon PPC improves through consistent testing and analysis. Small weekly improvements compound. Focus on relevant keywords, customer intent, and conversion rather than chasing clicks alone.
