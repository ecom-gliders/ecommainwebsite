## Why Most Amazon Sellers Struggle with High ACoS

Running Amazon PPC campaigns can feel like walking a tightrope. On one side, you want more visibility and higher sales. On the other, rising advertising costs can quickly eat into your profits. Many sellers believe that spending more money on ads automatically leads to more sales, but that’s one of the biggest myths in Amazon advertising.

The truth is that successful sellers don’t necessarily spend more. They spend smarter.

If your Advertising Cost of Sales (ACoS) keeps increasing while your profits shrink, don’t panic. The solution isn’t to stop advertising. Instead, it’s to optimize your campaigns using a proven Amazon PPC strategy that focuses on keyword quality, campaign structure, and data-driven decisions.

#### What Is Amazon PPC?

Amazon PPC (Pay-Per-Click) is Amazon’s advertising platform that allows sellers to promote their products directly within Amazon search results and product pages. You only pay when a shopper clicks on your ad, making it a cost-effective way to increase visibility.

Amazon currently offers three primary advertising options:

*   **Sponsored Products** for promoting individual listings.
*   **Sponsored Brands** to showcase your brand and multiple products.
*   **Sponsored Display** for retargeting shoppers and reaching audiences on and off Amazon.

For most beginners, Sponsored Products deliver the highest return because they target shoppers who are already searching for products similar to yours.

##### Why Amazon PPC Matters?

A well-managed PPC campaign helps you:

*   Increase product visibility.
*   Generate more sales.
*   Improve organic keyword rankings.
*   Launch new products faster.
*   Collect valuable customer search data.

However, without proper optimization, PPC campaigns can quickly become expensive.

#### Understanding ACoS:

Before improving your campaigns, you need to understand [Advertising Cost of Sales (ACoS).](https://ecomgliders.com/services/#service_5)

##### Formula:

**ACoS = (Advertising Spend ÷ Advertising Sales) × 100**

##### Example:

Advertising Spend

Sales Generated

ACoS

$150

$750

20%

This means you spent **20 cents on advertising for every dollar earned through PPC sales.**

##### Is a Lower ACoS Always Better?

Not necessarily.

Many new sellers chase the lowest possible ACoS, but that’s not always the smartest goal. A campaign with a 15% ACoS may generate very few sales, while another with a 28% ACoS could produce significantly higher profits because of greater sales volume.

Instead of asking, **“How can I lower my ACoS?”**, ask:

*   Is my campaign profitable?
*   Is my advertising increasing total sales?
*   Am I improving organic rankings?
*   Is my conversion rate healthy?

The objective isn’t simply to reduce ACoS. It’s to maximize profitability.

#### Master the Search Term Report:

Your Search Term Report is one of the most valuable tools in Amazon Advertising. It shows the exact words customers typed before clicking your ad.

Many beginners confuse **keywords** with **search terms**.

*   **Keyword:** The phrase you add to your campaign.
*   **Search Term:** The actual phrase a customer uses on Amazon.

This report helps you discover profitable long-tail keywords and identify wasteful searches.

### Review your Search Term Report every week and:

*   Add high-converting search terms to Exact Match campaigns.
*   Reduce bids for expensive keywords.
*   Add irrelevant terms as negative keywords.
*   Pause consistently unprofitable keywords.

Small weekly improvements often produce better long-term results than making large changes all at once.

#### The Hidden Hack: Improve Your Listing Before Raising Your Budget

Many sellers increase their PPC budget when sales slow down. In reality, the problem often lies in the product listing, not the advertising.

Before spending more, ask yourself:

*   Are your images high quality?
*   Does your main image stand out?
*   Is your title optimized with relevant keywords?
*   Do your bullet points highlight benefits?
*   Is your pricing competitive?
*   Do you have enough positive reviews?

A better listing improves your [conversion rate](https://www.reddit.com/r/FulfillmentByAmazon/comments/1joqe85/amazon_ppc_tips_for_beginners_and_advanced_things/), meaning more visitors become buyers. When conversions increase, Amazon rewards your ads with better performance, helping reduce your ACoS over time.

#### Weekly Amazon PPC Optimization Checklist:

Successful Amazon sellers don’t manage PPC once a month. They review campaigns consistently.

Use this weekly checklist:

*   Download the Search Term Report.
*   Add profitable search terms to Manual Exact campaigns.
*   Add irrelevant searches as negative keywords.
*   Lower bids on expensive keywords with poor conversions.
*   Increase bids slightly on profitable keywords.
*   Check click-through rate (CTR).
*   Monitor conversion rate (CVR).
*   Review ACoS and ROAS.
*   Pause keywords that continue to waste budget.
*   Analyze campaign performance before making major changes.

A disciplined optimization routine often outperforms frequent, impulsive adjustments.

#### Common Amazon PPC Mistakes:

Avoid these mistakes if you want to keep your advertising profitable.

**Using Only Broad Match:**

Broad Match is excellent for research but shouldn’t be your long-term strategy for every keyword.

**Ignoring Negative Keywords:**

Every irrelevant click costs money. Regularly adding negative keywords helps protect your advertising budget.

**Increasing Bids Too Quickly:**

Many sellers increase bids after only a few clicks. Wait until enough data has accumulated before making decisions.

**Optimizing Every Day:**

Amazon’s algorithm needs time to learn. Constant changes reset the learning process and can produce misleading results.

**Advertising Poor Listings:**

No advertising strategy can consistently overcome weak product images, unclear descriptions, or uncompetitive pricing.

#### Final Thoughts:

The best Amazon PPC hack isn’t a secret button or expensive software. It’s a disciplined optimization process built around data.

By separating keyword research from high-performing campaigns, reviewing Search Term Reports regularly, using negative keywords, optimizing bids, and improving your product listing, you can reduce wasted advertising spend while maintaining healthy sales growth.

Remember, Amazon PPC success doesn’t happen overnight. Campaigns improve through consistent testing, careful analysis, and ongoing optimization. Small improvements made every week often lead to significant long-term gains.

If you focus on relevant keywords, customer intent, and conversion optimization instead of simply chasing clicks, you’ll build campaigns that are both profitable and scalable.
