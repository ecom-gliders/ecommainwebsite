## Soft what a UPC is

A UPC (Universal Product Code) is typically a 12-digit identifier with a scannable barcode. Soft practice: distinct products, packs, sizes, and scents usually need distinct identifiers—**Assumed**; confirm for your category.

*   Soft claim: catalog integrity and duplicate reduction—**Assumed**  
*   Soft claim: clearer brand/product matching—**Assumed**
