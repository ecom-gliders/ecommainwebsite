## Negative Keywords on Amazon The Seller's Secret Weapon

## The Ultimate Guide to Maximizing Your Sales and Amazon Account Performance

### Introduction:

Amazon advrtising in 2026 is more competitive than ever.Sellers who ignore negative keywords often waste money on irrelevant clicks.Negative keywords help improve targeting and campaign profitability.

They are one of the most powerful tools for [Amazon PPC](https://www.sellerapp.com/blog/amazon-negative-keywords-guide/) optimization.What Are Amazon Negative Keywords? Negative keywords stop your ads from appearing in unrelated searches.They help filter out shoppers who are unlikely to purchase.

**Example:**

Selling premium watches?

Block terms like “cheap watches” or “free watches.”

**Why Negative Keywords Matter in 2026**

*   Reduce wasted ad spend.
*   Prevent irrelevant traffic from clicking your ads.
*   Improve conversion rates.
*   Increase campaign efficiency.
*   Help sellers focus budget on high-intent buyers.
*   Types of Negative Keywords on Amazon
*   Negative Exact Match
*   Blocks ads from appearing for an exact search term.
*   Best for removing specific low-performing keywords.
*   Negative Phrase Match
*   Blocks searches containing a certain phrase.
*   Helps eliminate broader irrelevant traffic.

**How to Find Negative Keywords:**

*   Open Amazon Search Term Reports regularly.

Identify keywords with:

*   High clicks
*   High spending
*   Low or zero conversions
*   Add those terms as negative keywords.
*   Example of PPC Optimization
*   Product: Wireless Gaming Mouse
*   Irrelevant search: “cheap office mouse”

Solution:

Add “cheap” and “office” as negative keywords.

**Best Practices for 2026:**

*   Review search term reports weekly.
*   Continuously update negative keyword lists.
*   Separate branded and non-branded campaigns.
*   Monitor ad performance regularly.
*   Test and optimize keyword targeting consistently.

### Conclusion:

Negative keywords are essential for Amazon sellers in 2026.They protect advertising budgets from irrelevant traffic.Smart PPC optimization leads to stronger conversions and better profits.Sellers who actively use negative keywords gain a competitive advantage in Amazon advertising.
