## What are Amazon negative keywords?

Amazon negative keywords are terms you block so your Sponsored ads do not show for those searches. They filter shoppers who are unlikely to buy, reduce wasted clicks, and help campaigns spend on higher-intent queries. In 2026, with higher CPCs in many categories, negatives are a core PPC control, not an optional cleanup.

Sellers who skip search-term hygiene often pay for irrelevant traffic. Negatives improve targeting precision and protect margin. ## Why negative keywords matter

*   Reduce wasted ad spend
*   Block irrelevant clicks
*   Improve conversion rate on remaining traffic
*   Keep budget on higher-intent buyers
*   Cleaner search-term data for optimization

## Types of negative keywords on Amazon

### Negative exact match

Blocks ads for that exact search term. Use it to remove specific low performers without cutting related phrases.

### Negative phrase match

Blocks searches that contain a phrase. Use it when a broader pattern is consistently irrelevant (for example, "cheap" or "free" for a premium offer).

**Example:** If you sell premium watches, consider blocking terms such as "cheap watches" or "free watches" when those shoppers never convert.

## How to find negative keywords

1.  Open Amazon search term reports on a regular cadence.
2.  Flag terms with high clicks or spend and low or zero conversions (after enough data).
3.  Confirm the term is truly irrelevant or unprofitable, not just early-stage.
4.  Add exact or phrase negatives at the campaign or ad group level as your structure requires.
5.  Recheck after changes so you do not over-block converting variants.

### Example

*   Product: wireless gaming mouse
*   Irrelevant search: "cheap office mouse"
*   Action: negate "office" and review "cheap" by match type and performance

Expected result: tighter targeting, less waste, clearer ROI on remaining keywords.

## Benefits of search term filtering

*   Cleaner campaign data

## Best practices for 2026

*   Review search term reports weekly (or more often on high spend)
*   Keep negative lists updated as discovery campaigns run
*   Separate branded and non-branded structures when volume allows
*   Monitor ACOS and conversion after each negation batch
*   Test carefully so you do not block profitable long-tail variants

## Bottom line

Negative keywords protect Amazon advertising budgets from irrelevant traffic. Active search-term filtering supports stronger conversions and clearer profit. Sellers who maintain negatives as a weekly habit usually waste less than sellers who only add them after major overspend.
