## Soft campaign type map

| Soft type | Soft role | Soft fit (**Assumed**) |
|-----------|-----------|------------------------|
| Sponsored Products | Promote individual ASINs in search and PDPs | Launches, keyword competition, conversion tests |
| Sponsored Brands | Logo, headline, multi-ASIN or storefront | Brand awareness, catalog cross-sell |
| Sponsored Display | Audience and product/retargeting placements | Remarketing, competitor PDP presence where allowed |
| Sponsored video (where available) | Video creative in eligible placements | Creative testing—eligibility **Requires confirmation** |

## Soft Sponsored Products

*   Keyword or product targeting  
*   Ads often resemble organic listings  
*   Soft use: measure conversion on search terms before heavy brand spend—**Proposed**  

## Soft Sponsored Brands

*   Brand assets and multi-product storytelling  
*   Soft use: after listing conversion baseline exists—**Assumed**  
*   Soft claim: Brand eligibility (Brand Registry and creative policy) **Requires confirmation**

## Soft Sponsored Display

*   On-Amazon and off-Amazon placements (where enabled)  
*   Soft use: retarget viewers or related-product audiences—**Assumed**  
*   Soft claim: off-Amazon reach and costs vary by marketplace—**Requires confirmation**

## Soft auto vs manual

| Soft mode | Soft use |
|-----------|----------|
| Auto | Discovery and search-term harvest |
| Manual | Control bids and exact/phrase targets after data |

Soft practice (**Proposed**): run both; graduate proven terms from auto into manual exact/phrase with negatives.

## Soft bidding notes

Amazon bidding styles commonly include dynamic down-only, dynamic up-and-down, and fixed—labels and behavior **Require confirmation** in Seller/Ads console.

| Soft style | Soft note |
|------------|-----------|
| Dynamic down only | Soft claim: can reduce bids on weaker conversion likelihood—**Assumed** |
| Dynamic up and down | Soft claim: more aggressive; can raise spend in peak periods—**Assumed** |
| Fixed | Soft claim: bid stays at your set value—**Assumed** |

Soft claim: “start up-and-down then switch down-only” is a heuristic, not a verified rule—**Proposed**. Soft twin bidding hygiene → [Amazon PPC campaign management](/amazon-ppc-campaign-management/).

## Soft selection sequence (**Proposed**)

1. Sponsored Products for conversion learning  
2. Add Sponsored Brands when brand assets and margin allow  
3. Add Sponsored Display for retargeting once traffic volume exists  
4. Keep auto for discovery and manual for control  
5. Soft twin keyword research → [Amazon PPC keyword research](/amazon-ppc-strategy-keyword-research-2025/)  

## Soft mistakes

*   Auto-only with no manual graduation  
*   Ignoring search-term and placement reports  
*   Over-bidding unproven keywords  
*   No negatives  
*   Mixing branded and non-branded without structure  
*   Soft claim: Maximum ROI framing overstates control—**Assumed**

## Soft optimization hygiene

*   Negatives for waste  
*   Bid moves on proven terms only after enough clicks  
*   Soft dayparting where tools support it—**Requires confirmation**
