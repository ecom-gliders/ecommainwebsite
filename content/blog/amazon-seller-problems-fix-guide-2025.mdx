### **Introduction:**

Selling on Amazon in 2025 remains one of the fastest ways to scale an e-commerce brand, but it’s not without challenges. From **Amazon Seller Problems** like inventory shortages and suspended listings to technical glitches in Seller Central, these issues can disrupt operations and drain profits.

With over 2 million active sellers competing worldwide, small mistakes can lead to big consequences. That’s why it’s critical to understand common problems Amazon sellers face and, more importantly, how to fix them quickly.

This guide is designed for brand owners, small-business leaders, and third-party sellers who want to stay ahead of issues and keep their Amazon business running smoothly. You’ll learn about the most pressing **Amazon seller issues** in 2025, practical fixes, preventive strategies, and expert insights to protect your account and boost growth.

### **Amazon Seller Central Issues:**

Amazon Seller Central is the brain of your business. From managing orders to [running](https://ecomgliders.com/amazon-fba-growth-scaling-mistakes/) ads, everything happens here. Unfortunately, it’s also where sellers face multiple hurdles.

#### **Common Amazon Seller Central Problems:**

*   **Amazon seller central login issues**
    *   Sellers often can’t log in due to password errors, 2FA authentication failures, or temporary account holds.  
        
*   **Amazon seller central login problems** on multiple devices  
    *   Login failures when switching between desktop and mobile.  
        
*   **Amazon seller central technical issues today**
    *   These include dashboards not loading, reports missing, or payment delays.  
        
*   **Amazon seller central outage**
    *   Rare but impactful, when entire systems go offline.  
        

#### **Fixes for Seller Central Issues:**

*   Clear your browser cache and cookies before logging in.  
    
*   Enable two-factor authentication through the Authenticator app instead of SMS (more reliable).  
    
*   Keep backup browsers like Firefox or Edge to bypass Chrome-related glitches.  
    
*   If an outage occurs, don’t panic—Amazon usually resolves these within hours. Document the downtime in case you need to request support.  
    

**Pro Tip:** Always maintain offline backups of product data, sales reports, and listings. If Seller Central goes down, you won’t be blind.

### **Amazon FBA Problems & Fixes:**

FBA (Fulfillment by Amazon) is a lifeline for millions of sellers—but it’s also a source of constant stress.

#### **Common Amazon FBA Issues:**

*   **Lost inventory** during shipping or inside warehouses.  
    
*   **Inbound shipment delays** causing stockouts.  
    
*   **Amazon FBA problems** with customer returns, often [refunded](https://www.amazonsellers.attorney/blog/amazon-seller-account-deactivated-vs-suspended-2025-expert-guide?srsltid=AfmBOopU172KHg1EsfK5k1Shq1MIuSQBLMtS0hTtA7ZWN1DKl6CRieQ-) without product checks.  
    
*   **High storage fees** due to overstocking.  
    

#### **Fixes:**

*   **Lost Inventory:** File reimbursement claims promptly via Seller Central. Amazon reimburses sellers for lost or damaged units if tracked properly.  
    
*   **Delayed Shipments:** Always include box-level content information. It speeds up Amazon’s receiving process.  
    
*   **Customer Returns Abuse:** Track repeat offenders and report fraudulent behavior. Amazon may block abusive buyers.  
    
*   **Storage Fees:** Use inventory forecasting tools to send the right quantity of stock. Avoid seasonal overstocking.  
    

**Pro Tip:** Use third-party inventory management tools like SoStocked or RestockPro to predict sales trends and avoid surprises.

### **Amazon Inventory Issues:**

Inventory is the backbone of your Amazon business. If you don’t manage it, everything else falls apart.

#### **Common Amazon Inventory Problems:**

*   **Stockouts** leading to lost Buy Box and lower rankings.  
    
*   **Overstocks** draining profit due to storage fees.  
    
*   **Stranded inventory** stuck in the warehouse because listings are inactive.  
    
*   **Amazon inventory issues** like discrepancies between actual stock and what Amazon shows.  
    

#### **Fixes:**

*   **Set smart reorder points**: Always reorder when stock drops to cover at least 30 days of sales.  
    
*   **Audit stranded inventory** regularly in Seller Central.  
    
*   **Balance stock** by splitting between FBA and FBM (Fulfilled by Merchant).  
    

**Use Inventory Performance Index (IPI):** Keep it above Amazon’s threshold to avoid storage restrictions.  

### **Amazon Listing Problems:**

Your listings are your storefront. When they go wrong, sales collapse.

#### **Common Amazon Listing Problems:**

*   **Suppressed listings** due to incomplete data or policy violations.  
    
*   **Hijacked listings** by unauthorized sellers.  
    
*   **Incorrect attributes** such as [size,](https://ecomgliders.com/top-amazon-seller-growth-hacks-2025/) color, or brand.  
    
*   **Image errors** leading to suppressed search visibility.  
    

#### **Fixes:**

*   Regularly check the **Suppressed Listings Dashboard**.  
    
*   Use Amazon Brand Registry to protect intellectual property.  
    
*   Follow Amazon’s image and copy guidelines strictly.  
    
*   Report hijackers immediately via “Report a Violation” in Seller Central.  
    

**Pro Tip:** A+ Content (Enhanced Brand Content) not only improves conversions but also prevents suppression due to missing details

### **Amazon Seller Identity Verification Problems:**

Without verification, you can’t sell. Yet many sellers face **Amazon seller identity verification problems**.

#### **Common Causes:**

*   Expired IDs or mismatched documents.  
    
*   Low-quality scans or altered PDFs.  
    
*   Inconsistent business details (name, address, tax ID).  
    

#### **Fixes:**

*   Submit clear, [high-resolution](https://myamazonguy.com/amazon-product-launch/mistakes-of-new-amazon-sellers/) scans.  
    
*   Use documents less than 90 days old.  
    
*   Ensure business registration, bank, and tax details match exactly.  
    

**Pro Tip:** Never Photoshop documents. Even small edits can lead to permanent account suspension.

### **Amazon Seller App Encountered a Problem:**

For mobile sellers, the **Amazon seller app encountered a problem** message is frustrating.

#### **Fixes:**

*   Update the app frequently.  
    
*   Clear mobile cache and restart.  
    
*   Switch to desktop for urgent tasks.  
    

### **Amazon Seller Outage & Technical Issues:**

Amazon’s systems occasionally fail. A sudden **Amazon seller outage** can disrupt orders, listings, or ads.

#### **Fixes:**

*   Monitor outages on AWS service health dashboards.  
    
*   Pause ad [campaigns](https://sequencecommerce.com/amazon-seller-faqs/) during outages to prevent wasted spend.  
    

Keep offline backups of product titles, SKUs, and ASINs.  

### **Problems with Amazon Third Party Sellers:**

Not all problems are technical—many are competitive.

#### **Common Problems with Amazon Third Party Sellers:**

*   Price undercutting and Buy Box wars.  
    
*   Hijacked or counterfeit listings.  
    
*   Manipulated reviews and fake ratings.  
    

#### **Fixes:**

*   Use automated repricing tools to stay competitive.  
    
*   Enroll in Brand Registry and Transparency program to block counterfeiters.  
    

Monitor reviews with software and report suspicious patterns.  

### **Problems with Selling on Amazon (Hidden Challenges):**

Some problems are less visible but still dangerous:

*   **Rising Fees:** FBA storage, referral, and advertising costs are eating profits.  
    
*   **Increased Competition:** More sellers means tougher battles.  
    
*   **Policy Strictness:** Even minor [violations](https://www.velocitysellers.com/2025/05/30/amazon-seller-problems/) can lead to suspension.  
    

**Solution:** Diversify beyond Amazon. Build a Shopify or direct-to-consumer store to reduce dependency.
