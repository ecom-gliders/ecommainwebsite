### What is ACOS & Why Does It Matter?

ACOS (Advertising Cost of Sale) measures the efficiency of your Amazon ads. It’s calculated as:

```formula
ACOS = (Ad Spend ÷ Ad Revenue) × 100
```

```checklist
- **High ACOS (30%+)** = You’re spending too much on ads relative to sales, hurting profitability.
- **Low ACOS (<15%)** = Efficient spending, but you may be missing out on potential sales.
- **Optimal ACOS (15-25%)** = The sweet spot where ads are profitable while maximizing growth.
```

**Lowering ACOS doesn’t mean reducing sales—it means optimizing for better ROI.**

### 5 Proven Strategies to Reduce ACOS & Boost Sales

#### 1. Optimize Your Keyword Strategy

| Strategy | Action Steps | Expected Impact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target high-intent keywords | Use "buy \[product\]" instead of "best \[product\]" | Higher conversion rates |
| Add negative keywords | Exclude irrelevant searches (e.g., "cheap," "free") | Lower wasted ad spend |
| Use long-tail keywords | Focus on low-competition, high-converting phrases | Lower CPC, better ACOS |

#### 2. Improve Your Product Listing for Better Conversions

```checklist
- **High-quality images & videos** → Boosts CTR & conversions
- **Compelling bullet points** → Highlight benefits, not just features
- **Strong reviews & ratings** → Social proof increases trust
```

#### 3. Adjust Bids Strategically

| Bid Strategy | When to Use | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Lower bids on low-performers | High ACOS, low conversion keywords | Reduce wasted spend |
| Increase bids on top keywords | High-converting, low-ACOS terms | More sales at better efficiency |
| Use Amazon’s Dynamic Bidding | Let Amazon optimize bids in real-time | Smarter ad spend |

#### 4. Leverage Sponsored Brands & DSP Ads

```checklist
- **Sponsored Brands (Headline Search Ads)** → Build brand awareness & lower ACOS
- **Amazon DSP (Retargeting Ads)** → Bring back lost shoppers at a lower cost
```

#### 5. Monitor & Optimize Campaigns Weekly

6.  **Check Search Term Reports**→ Find new keywords & irrelevant terms
7.  **Pause underperforming ads**→ Stop bleeding ad spend
8.  **A/B test ad creatives**→ Improve CTR & conversion rates
