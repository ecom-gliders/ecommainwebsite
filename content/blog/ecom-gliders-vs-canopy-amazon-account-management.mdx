## How do eCom Gliders and Canopy Management compare?

eCom Gliders and Canopy Management are both full-service Amazon growth partners that publicly manage advertising, listings, and account operations for brands that need outside execution. The useful comparison is channel emphasis, how tightly PPC connects to listing conversion and operations, reporting cadence, and commercial fit—not stereotypes like "tactical vs enterprise."

An older draft on this URL framed eCom Gliders as aggressive short-term PPC only and Canopy as the sole profit-first option. That framing was inaccurate. eCom Gliders connects PPC with listing conversion and catalog health so spend supports profitable growth. Canopy Management publicly markets full-service Amazon account management across advertising, listings, inventory, brand support, customer service, and related operations, including Seller Central and Vendor Central messaging (Observed on canopymanagement.com, 2026-10-30).

## How to choose between two full-service Amazon agencies

Ask both partners the same questions:

*   Who owns the account day to day, and are there team handoffs?
*   How are PPC changes coordinated with listings, inventory, and suppressions?
*   Do you need Seller Central only, Vendor Central, or hybrid support?
*   What profit metrics do you report besides ACOS?
*   What is included in the retainer versus add-on?

**Lean toward eCom Gliders when you want:**

*   Amazon-first PPC, listing, and catalog execution from one agency
*   Audit-led prioritization before scaling spend
*   Clear connection between ad waste cleanup and conversion creative

**Evaluate Canopy Management when you want:**

*   Publicly marketed end-to-end Amazon operations including inventory and customer service in one conversation
*   Seller and Vendor Central coverage messaging
*   Broader marketplace / omnichannel options as part of the same vendor shortlist

There is no universal winner. Match the written scope to your account type and bottleneck.
