### **How to Scale Amazon Business from $10K to $100K (The Roadmap ):**

Scaling is a **step-by-step process**. Here’s a roadmap you can follow:

#### **Step 1: Increase Average Order Value (AOV)**

*   Bundle products (2-pack, 3-pack).  
    
*   Add premium product options.  
    
*   Use upselling inside listings (e.g., “Buy with…”).  
    

#### **Step 2: Improve Conversion Rate (CR)**

*   Use **split testing** for titles/images.  
    
*   Add **comparison charts** (show why your product is better).  
    
*   Add **lifestyle photos** for emotional triggers.  
    

#### **Step 3: Boost Organic Rankings**

*   Run PPC on main keywords until you rank organically.  
    
*   Optimize backend keywords with high-volume terms.  
    
*   Use **search-find-buy campaigns** to influence ranking.  
    

#### **Step 4: Reinvest Aggressively**

*   25–30% of profits should go into ads.  
    
*   Upgrade packaging → higher perceived value.  
    
*   Spend on brand design (logos, inserts, storefront).  
    

### **Amazon PPC Scaling and Ads Optimization Explained :**

Amazon PPC is your growth engine. Without it, scaling is slow.

#### **Mini-Guide: 7 Steps to Scale PPC in 2025:**

*   **Start with Auto Campaigns** → Collect data.  
    
*   **Move to Manual Campaigns** → Exact & Phrase match.  
    
*   **Harvest Keywords** → Use data from auto campaigns.  
    
*   **Optimize Bids Weekly** → Cut waste, double down on winners.  
    
*   **Add Negative Keywords** → Remove irrelevant traffic.  
    
*   **Expand Campaign Types** → Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, Video Ads.  
    
*   **Scale Budgets Gradually** → 20–30% increase weekly on profitable campaigns.
