## Soft conversion and AOV levers

*   Bundles and premium options when unit economics work  
*   Title/image tests; comparison and lifestyle creatives when compliant  
*   Soft claim: search-find-buy style tactics can violate policy if manipulated—**Requires confirmation**; prefer compliant velocity and conversion work  

## Soft PPC while scaling

1. Auto → harvest search terms  
2. Manual phrase/exact → scale proven terms  
3. Weekly negatives and bid hygiene  
4. Raise budgets gradually only when contribution margin holds  
5. Soft claim: “25–30% of profits into ads” or “20–30% weekly budget raises” are not universal rules—**Requires confirmation**

## Soft inventory and brand

*   Forecast inbound before stockouts interrupt rank continuity—**Assumed**  
*   Brand Registry / A+ / Store when eligible  
*   Soft claim: Subscribe & Save and influencer programs are optional capacity bets; “5–10x ROI” claims are unverified—**Not yet tested**  
*   Soft claim: tool brand names in older marketing copy are not endorsements here  

## Soft checklist before pushing spend (**Proposed**)

1. Contribution margin after fees and ads is known  
2. Listing converts before aggressive budget raises  
3. Inventory cover matches lead time  
4. Account health is stable  
5. Owner bandwidth or agency cadence exists for weekly hygiene
