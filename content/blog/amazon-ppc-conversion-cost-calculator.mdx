## How to use the Amazon PPC Conversion Cost Calculator

When selling on Amazon, managing advertising costs is essential for profitability. PPC campaigns can be a significant expense, and understanding how those costs translate into sales helps you decide where to invest. The Amazon Conversion Cost Calculator breaks down the costs and profit associated with your advertising efforts so you can make clearer PPC decisions.

### Purpose of the calculator

The Amazon Conversion Cost Calculator surfaces the metrics that matter when you evaluate PPC performance:

```checklist
Cost per PPC Session | How much each click on your advertised product costs.
Number of Sessions per Conversion | The average number of clicks needed to achieve a sale.
Cost per Conversion | The cost associated with converting a click into a sale.
Profit per Unit | The profit made from each unit sold, not accounting for PPC costs.
Net Profit or Loss per Unit | The final profit or loss per unit sold after accounting for PPC costs.
```

### Using the calculator: step-by-step

```steps
Input your data | Fill in PPC Spend ($), Sessions (Clicks), CVR Conversion Rate (%), Sale Price ($), Amazon Fees ($), and Cost of Goods Sold ($) using figures from your Amazon PPC reports and unit economics.
Calculate | Click Calculate. The tool processes the inputs and shows results in the output section.
Review the results | Check Cost per PPC Session, Sessions per Conversion, Cost per Conversion, Profit per Unit, and Net Profit or Loss per Unit. Use these to judge whether bids, listings, or pricing need changes.
Export and copy results | Use Export to CSV or Copy to Clipboard when you need the numbers in a spreadsheet or report.
```

### Tips for optimization

```tip
TITLE: Reduce PPC spend
If your Cost per Conversion is high, tighten targeting, improve relevance, or lower bids before increasing budget.
```

```tip
TITLE: Improve conversion rate
Strengthen product images, titles, and descriptions so more clicks convert into orders.
```

```tip
TITLE: Adjust pricing
If profit margins are thin after PPC, revisit price, fees, and cost of goods before scaling spend.
```

### Conclusion

The Amazon Conversion Cost Calculator helps sellers using PPC campaigns on Amazon understand each component of advertising spend. Used regularly, it supports clearer bid decisions, better campaign reviews, and more sustainable unit economics.
