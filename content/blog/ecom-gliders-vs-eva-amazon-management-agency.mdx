## How do eCom Gliders and Eva Commerce compare?

eCom Gliders is a full-service Amazon seller growth agency that executes PPC, listing optimization, creative, catalog, and account management. Eva Commerce (often shortened to Eva) publicly positions as a full-service ecommerce performance agency managing Amazon alongside Shopify, TikTok Shop, and related marketplace expansion, with human specialists plus Eva Intelligence tooling (Observed on eva.guru, 2026-10-30). Both can manage Amazon accounts. The useful comparison is Amazon-first depth versus multi-channel scope—not "agency vs virtual assistants."

An older draft on this URL incorrectly described EVA as an "Ecommerce Virtual Assistants" support provider. That label does not match Eva Commerce's current public positioning as a managed growth agency. Competitor claims and pricing change; verify current offers directly.

## How to choose

Ask both partners the same questions:

*   Do you need Amazon-only depth or Amazon plus Shopify/TikTok under one vendor?
*   Who owns weekly Seller Central / Ads execution versus strategy only?
*   How are PPC changes coordinated with listing conversion and inventory?
*   What reporting cadence covers ACOS/TACoS, catalog health, and suppressions?
*   What is included in the written retainer versus add-ons?

**Lean toward eCom Gliders when you want:**

*   Amazon-first PPC, listing, and catalog execution from one agency
*   Audit-led prioritization before scaling spend
*   Clear connection between ad waste cleanup and conversion creative

**Evaluate Eva Commerce when you want:**

*   One partner conversation across Amazon and other ecommerce channels
*   Public full-service marketplace management plus omnichannel messaging
*   Technology-assisted operations with named growth-specialist ownership

There is no universal winner. Match the written scope to your channel mix and internal bandwidth.
