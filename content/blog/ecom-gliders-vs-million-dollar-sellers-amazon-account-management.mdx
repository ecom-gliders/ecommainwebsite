## How do eCom Gliders and Million Dollar Sellers compare?

eCom Gliders is a full-service Amazon seller growth agency that executes PPC, listing optimization, creative, catalog, and account management. Million Dollar Sellers (MDS) publicly positions as an exclusive ecommerce community for vetted high-revenue brand founders, focused on peer networking, events, coaching resources, and member collaboration—not day-to-day Amazon account management (Observed on mds.co, 2026-10-30). They are different categories. One is done-for-you agency execution; the other is peer community membership.

## What Million Dollar Sellers publicly offers

Based on MDS public positioning (Observed on mds.co, 2026-10-30), Million Dollar Sellers markets:

*   A vetted community for ecommerce brand founders at higher revenue bands
*   Peer networking, chapters/events, and member collaboration
*   Live learning, resources, and operator-to-operator knowledge sharing
*   A model where members remain the operators of their brands

Public member-count and combined-revenue claims are marketing statements (Requires confirmation before treating as audited proof). Membership criteria and fees change; verify directly with MDS.

MDS is not a substitute for an Amazon PPC and listing team unless you separately hire an agency or in-house operators to implement what you learn.

## Agency vs community: how to choose

**Choose eCom Gliders if you:**

*   Need specialists to run campaigns and improve listings for you
*   Want ads, creative, and catalog work coordinated under one Amazon agency
*   Prefer audit-led prioritization and structured reporting
*   Do not have bandwidth to implement peer advice alone

**Consider evaluating Million Dollar Sellers if you:**

*   Already run a substantial ecommerce brand and meet membership criteria
*   Want vetted peer networking and operator community access
*   Prefer to keep execution in-house or with your own vendors
*   Accept that results still depend on your team's follow-through

Some founders use both: a community for peer learning and an agency for execution. The mismatch to avoid is paying for networking when you needed managed PPC—or hiring an agency when you only wanted peer access.
