## Soft optimization pillars

*   **Keywords:** branded defense, category, long-tail; refresh from search-term reports  
*   **Negatives:** cut irrelevant clicks  
*   **Bids / placements:** raise only where CVR supports cost  
*   **Creatives / listing:** CTR and conversion before scaling spend  
*   **Budget:** move spend from waste to proven segments gradually  

Soft claim: PPC can support sales velocity that helps organic visibility; it does not guarantee rank—**Assumed**.

## Soft campaign setup (**Proposed**)

1. Define objective: launch learning, profitability, or brand defense  
2. Choose auto for discovery and manual for control  
3. Structure portfolios → campaigns → ad groups cleanly  
4. Balance broad / phrase / exact; graduate winners to exact  
5. Soft bidding: down-only while learning; mix fixed/dynamic by role—**Assumed**
