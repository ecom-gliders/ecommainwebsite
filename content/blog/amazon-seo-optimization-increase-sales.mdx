## What Amazon SEO means here

Soft claim: listings that match query relevance and convert better tend to sustain visibility better than ads alone—**Assumed**. Soft claim: informal labels like “A9” are historical seller shorthand, not a verified current Amazon product name in this guide—**Requires confirmation**.

## Soft SEO vs PPC

| Soft lever | Soft role |
|------------|-----------|
| Listing SEO | Durable organic presence when relevance and conversion hold |
| PPC | Controlled discovery, launches, seasonality, competitive defense |
| Combined | PPC can fund learning and velocity while SEO compounds—**Assumed** |

Soft claim: “SEO always outperforms ads” and “ads disappear the day budget stops” are oversimplifications—organic rank also moves with competition and stock—**Assumed**. Soft claim: treat “without burning money on ads” as a soft goal, not a guarantee—**Assumed**.

## Soft listing core

*   Title: brand + product type + key attribute + size/variant when natural  
*   Bullets: benefits tied to buyer problems; light keyword use, not stuffing  
*   Description / A+: clear story and comparison when eligible  
*   Backend search terms: synonyms and gaps not already covered; avoid duplicates  
*   Soft claim: byte limits and indexing rules change—confirm in Seller Central—**Requires confirmation**  
*   Soft claim: using competitor brand names in backend can create policy risk—**Requires confirmation**; prefer compliant synonyms  

## Soft keyword research (**Proposed**)

1. Amazon autocomplete and related search cues  
2. Competitor listing gap scan  
3. Brand Analytics where eligible  
4. Prioritize a mix of primary and long-tail terms you can convert  

Soft practice: readable titles beat stuffed titles for CTR—**Assumed**.

## Soft images and A+

*   Compliant main image; supporting lifestyle and infographic shots as allowed  
*   Soft claim: CTR and conversion influence performance signals—**Assumed**  
*   Soft claim: “at least 7 images” is a soft practice target, not a verified ranking rule—**Requires confirmation**  
*   A+ Content when Brand Registry allows—soft conversion help, not guaranteed lift—**Assumed**  

## Soft reviews, price, and mistakes

*   Policy-safe review requests only; Vine where eligible—**Requires confirmation**  
*   Soft claim: ratings support conversion; exact “4.5+ required” thresholds are not verified here—**Assumed**  
*   Competitive pricing and coupons/deals are optional margin decisions  
*   Soft mistakes: keyword stuffing, empty backend, weak images, copied competitor copy, no review ops
