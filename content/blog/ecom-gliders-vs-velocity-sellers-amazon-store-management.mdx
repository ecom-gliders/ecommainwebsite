## How do eCom Gliders and Velocity Sellers compare for Amazon store management?

eCom Gliders and Velocity Sellers are both full-service Amazon agencies that manage growth work such as listings, advertising, and account operations for sellers who need outside execution. Neither is a passive "set and forget" store-ownership product. The useful comparison is scope emphasis, how tightly PPC and listing work connect, reporting style, and how involved you want to stay as the brand owner.

This page corrects an older draft that incorrectly framed eCom Gliders as wholesale automation for absentee investors. eCom Gliders is an Amazon PPC and seller growth agency helping sellers since 2018, with in-house teams across advertising, content, creative, catalog, and account management. Velocity Sellers publicly positions as a full-service Amazon seller agency covering account management, listings, PPC, inventory, and related channel work (Observed on velocitysellers.com, 2026-10-30). Competitor details can change; verify current offers directly before hiring.

## Service model overview

| Area | eCom Gliders | Velocity Sellers (public positioning) |
|------|--------------|----------------------------------------|
| Model | Full-service Amazon agency with hands-on specialist execution | Full-service Amazon seller agency with dedicated account management |
| Core work | PPC, listing optimization, content, visual creative, catalog, account management | Account management, listings/SEO, PPC, inventory/logistics, account health (Observed) |
| Channel focus | Amazon-first growth systems for sellers and brands | Amazon Seller/Vendor Central with public multi-channel expansion messaging (Observed) |
| Client involvement | Collaborative; brand owns strategy decisions while eCom Gliders executes | Collaborative account management with standing reporting cadence (Observed) |
| Best starting point | Brands that need PPC, listing, and catalog work tied together | Brands evaluating full account operations plus inventory and health coverage |

Neither model replaces your ownership of the Amazon account. Both are service partnerships, not store purchases.

## Where Velocity Sellers publicly focuses

Based on Velocity Sellers' public website positioning (Observed 2026-10-30), the agency markets:

*   Dedicated account managers for Seller Central and Vendor Central
*   Listings, Amazon SEO, PPC (including DSP messaging on public pages)
*   Inventory and FBA logistics support
*   Account health, case management, and reporting
*   Expansion messaging into other marketplaces for some engagements

Treat that list as marketing positioning, not an independent audit of every client delivery. Ask Velocity Sellers for current scope, SLAs, and pricing during evaluation.

## Pricing and engagement style

Neither agency should be assumed to publish a universal public price list. Engagements are typically scoped after an audit or discovery call. Assumed patterns in the market include monthly retainers, project fees, and sometimes ad-management fees tied to spend. Confirm commercials in writing for either partner.

eCom Gliders starts many evaluations with a [free Amazon audit](/free-audit/) so priorities are based on account evidence rather than a generic package pitch.

## Reporting and decision rights

Useful questions for either agency:

*   Who owns Seller Central access and final commercial decisions?
*   How often do you review ACOS/TACoS, organic rank, inventory, and account health together?
*   Are listing and PPC changes coordinated, or handled in silos?
*   What happens when a listing is suppressed or policy risk appears?

eCom Gliders emphasizes connecting PPC waste reduction with listing conversion so paid and organic work reinforce each other. See [case studies](/case-studies/) for audit-led examples.

*   Amazon-first PPC, listing, and catalog execution from one agency
