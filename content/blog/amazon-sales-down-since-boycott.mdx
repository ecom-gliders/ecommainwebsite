## Are Amazon sales down since the boycott?

Soft short answer: **a short boycott window is unlikely to prove a verified company-wide sales decline in Amazon’s disclosed results**—and seller ASINs can still drop for other reasons—**Assumed**. Soft claim: “sales down since the boycott” needs a defined window, metric, and baseline. ## Soft how to answer “down since the boycott”

| Question | Soft requirement |
|----------|------------------|
| Down vs what? | Prior weeks, YoY, or prior quarter |
| Which sales? | Company net sales vs your ordered product sales |
| Which boycott? | Dates and geography |
| Attribution? | Boycott vs seasonality, ads, stock, competition |

Soft claim: without those, viral “Amazon is down” posts are not verified analysis.

## Soft company lens

Amazon reports diversified revenue (retail/marketplace, subscriptions, advertising, AWS). Soft claim: short consumer retail pauses often leave cloud and ads less touched—**Assumed**. Soft claim: moderated growth rates are not the same as absolute sales going down—**Assumed**. Soft practice: read the latest Amazon 10-Q/10-K or earnings release for the periods that overlap your boycott dates—**Requires confirmation**.

## Soft seller lens

If *your* sales fell after boycott headlines:

1. Compare sessions and units for the boycott week vs prior and YoY weeks  
2. Split organic vs paid  
3. Check Buy Box, OOS, and listing health  
4. Compare peer ASINs in the same subcategory  
5. Only then weigh niche sentiment sensitivity  

Soft claim: listing, inventory, and ads still explain most account drops—**Assumed**.

## Soft why short boycotts rarely show as “Amazon is down”

*   Habit and Prime convenience  
*   Timing shifts (buy before/after) rather than permanent exits—**Assumed**  
*   Macro noise (inflation, FX, rates) can dwarf a one-week protest in filings—**Assumed**  
*   Intention–behavior gap on social campaigns—**Assumed**  

## Bottom line

Soft claim: available public signals do not support treating every boycott news cycle as a verified Amazon-wide sales collapse. Soft claim: answer the question with filings for the company and Business Reports for your brand.
