# International Expansion: Selling on Amazon Global Marketplaces in 2025

## Selling on Amazon Global 2025

##### Introduction

Expanding your Amazon business across global marketplaces in 2025, or *selling on Amazon Global*, is a powerful way to tap into a combined monthly user base of over **321 million** and nearly **12 million daily orders**. As Amazon adds new markets like Ireland, UAE, Brazil, and Saudi Arabia, the potential grows—but so do complexities around localization, logistics, and compliance. This guide will help you navigate the process and scale confidently.

![International Expansion: Selling on Amazon Global Marketplaces in 2025](/images/blog/Getting-Started-Key-Steps-to-Go-Global-ea627639.png)

#### Why Expand Internationally?

**1\. Massive Customer Reach**

*   Access a global pool of 300M+ monthly shoppers
    
*   Enter emerging markets—Brazil, UAE, Saudi Arabia—where Amazon usage is rapidly growing
    

**2\. Reduced Competition & Greater Brand Exposure**

*   International marketplaces often have *less saturation* compared to the U.S.
    
*   Helps diversify market risks and build a global brand presence
    

**3\. Leverage Amazon’s Infrastructure**

*   Use FBA/MCF across both domestic and overseas marketplaces without setting up new logistics
    
*   Enjoy fast delivery and Prime-eligible status overseas
    

**4\. Boost Sales & Revenue**

*   Amazon’s global revenue exceeded **$142 B** in international marketplaces in 2024 (9% year-over-year), with **Q1 2025** sales at **$33.5B** (+5%)
    
*   International selling is increasingly driving growth for ambitious sellers
    

#### Getting Started: Key Steps to Go Global

##### 1\. Choose Target Market(s)

*   Start with **high-volume**, English-speaking regions (UK, Canada, Australia)
    
*   Gradually expand to **emerging markets** where competition is lower
    

##### 2\. Align Your Account

*   Use [Amazon Global Selling](https://sell.amazon.com/programs/global-selling) to unify your Seller Central account & simplify onboarding (supports 16+ marketplaces)
    
*   Be aware of additional registration steps, fees, and VAT obligations
    

##### 3\. Localize Listings

*   Translate titles, descriptions, and units into the local language
    
*   Adjust pricing to reflect local purchasing power and costs
    

##### 4\. Set Up Fulfillment

*   Use **FBA** to ship inventory into local fulfillment centers
    
*   Alternatively, utilize [Amazon Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF)](https://sell.amazon.com/programs/mcf) to fulfill across channels with unbranded packaging
    
*   Consider dual fulfillment—localized stock plus domestic shipping
    

##### 5\. Optimize Marketing & Ads

*   Tailor PPC campaigns based on regional demand, cultural preferences, and keywords
    
*   Utilize Amazon Advertising tools and DSP for broader reach
    

##### 6\. Manage Currency, Taxes, & Compliance

*   Register for VAT/GST in each country
    
*   Monitor FX fluctuations and implement protective pricing strategies
    
*   Understand import regulations, duties, and reporting requirements
