Soft claim: manipulated ratings can mislead buyers and distort competition—**Assumed**. Soft claim: Amazon may remove reviews and take account action when policy is breached—severity **Requires confirmation**.

Commonly referenced themes include bans on incentives for reviews, reviewing your own products, review exchanges, and multi-account rating schemes. ## Soft how to report suspected abuse (**Proposed**; UI changes)

**On the product page:** open the review → Report abuse → describe the concern → submit.

**Via Seller Central:** Help → Contact Us → report a violation path → attach screenshots, dates, and reviewer identifiers when available.

Soft claim: reporting does not guarantee removal, ranking recovery, or sales lift—**Assumed**.

## Soft signals that may warrant a closer look (**Assumed**)

*   Generic praise with no product detail  
*   Sudden review volume spikes  
*   Thin reviewer history  
*   Internally contradictory text  

Soft claim: signals are not proof of fraud—**Assumed**.
