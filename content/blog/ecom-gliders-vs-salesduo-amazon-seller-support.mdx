The useful comparison is channel emphasis, how tightly PPC connects to listing conversion, Seller versus Vendor Central operations, and commercial fit—not stereotypes like "structured vs aggressive."

An older draft on this URL framed SalesDuo as mainly aggressive PPC with reactive compliance. ## How to choose

Ask both agencies the same questions:

*   Do you need Seller Central only, Vendor Central, or hybrid support?
*   How are PPC changes coordinated with listing conversion and inventory?
*   Who owns final commercial decisions and Seller Central access?
*   What reporting cadence covers ACOS/TACoS, catalog health, and suppressions?
*   What is included versus add-on in the written retainer?

**Lean toward eCom Gliders when you want:**

*   Amazon-first PPC, listing, and catalog execution from one agency
*   Audit-led prioritization before scaling spend
*   Clear connection between ad waste cleanup and conversion creative

**Evaluate SalesDuo when you want:**

*   Publicly marketed Seller and Vendor Central operations in one partner conversation
*   Strong Vendor ops / EDI / chargeback support messaging
*   AI platform plus specialist team positioning for day-to-day Amazon management

There is no universal winner. Match the written scope to your account type and bottleneck.
