## Are Amazon sales increasing?

Soft short answer: **for sellers, “increasing” means your Business Reports show higher ordered product sales (and healthy contribution margin) versus a clear baseline**—not a viral company headline. Soft twin: company/segment framing → [are Amazon sales growing](/are-amazon-sales-growing/); drops → [decline in Amazon sales](/decline-in-amazon-sales/). Related: [Amazon PPC](/amazon-ppc/) and [free Amazon audit](/free-audit/).

![Checking whether Amazon seller sales are increasing YoY.](/images/blog/ChatGPT-Image-Nov-22-2025-08_27_42-PM-1024x683-fc993bd0.png)

## Soft company vs seller question

| Question | Soft place to look |
|----------|--------------------|
| Is Amazon Inc. revenue rising? | Latest Amazon filings / earnings—**Requires confirmation** |
| Are *my* Amazon sales increasing? | Seller Central: sales, sessions, conversion, ads |
| Is my *category* rising? | Peer ASINs + seasonality |

Soft claim: eCom Gliders focuses on seller performance; company tables on blogs go stale and must be verified.

## Soft seller checklist (primary)

Compare the same calendar weeks YoY and the last 4–8 weeks vs prior period:

1. Ordered product sales ($) and units  
2. Sessions and unit session percentage  
3. Organic vs paid share  
4. Buy Box % and OOS days  
5. TACOS / ACoS if ads are material  
6. Refund rate (growth that refunds is not healthy growth)  

Soft claim: sales up with margin collapsing is not the win operators want—**Assumed**.

## Soft why company growth can diverge from your ASIN

*   Category competition and price wars  
*   Inventory or Buy Box loss  
*   Ad auction inflation  
*   Listing quality gaps  
*   Seasonality (your niche off-peak while Amazon totals rise)  

## Soft levers when you want sales increasing

*   Fix conversion blockers (images, A+ content, reviews, price)  
*   Stabilize inventory before scaling spend  
*   Structure [Amazon PPC](/amazon-ppc/) by intent, not set-and-forget  
*   Expand only after unit economics clear  

## Bottom line

Soft claim: answer “are Amazon sales increasing?” at the **account** level with YoY Business Reports; use filings only for the company question. Soft claim: platform growth does not auto-increase every listing.
