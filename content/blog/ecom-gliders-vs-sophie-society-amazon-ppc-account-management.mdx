## How to choose between two full-service Amazon agencies

Ask both partners the same questions:

*   Who owns Seller Central access and final commercial decisions?
*   How are PPC changes coordinated with listing, inventory, and suppressions?
*   What profit metrics do you report besides ACOS (for example TACoS, contribution margin)?
*   What does onboarding, weekly optimization, and monthly review look like?
*   What is excluded from the retainer?

**Lean toward eCom Gliders when you want:**

*   Amazon-first PPC, listing, and catalog execution from one agency
*   Audit-led prioritization before scaling spend
*   Clear connection between ad waste cleanup and conversion creative

**Evaluate Sophie Society when you want:**

*   Publicly marketed end-to-end Amazon channel management with dedicated strategist messaging
*   Advertising coordinated with catalog, content, inventory, and promotions in one vendor conversation
*   Verified Partner positioning as part of your vendor shortlist criteria

There is no universal winner. Choose the partner whose written scope, proof, and communication cadence match your brand stage.
