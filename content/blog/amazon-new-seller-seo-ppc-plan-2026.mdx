## New Amazon Seller? Here's Your First 90 Day SEO and PPC Plan

Starting on Amazon feels like walking into a giant digital jungle. Millions of products. Thousands of sellers. And somewhere in that chaos, your listing is supposed to survive. Sounds dramatic because it kinda is.

But here’s the thing: Amazon gives new sellers a tiny golden window called the “**honeymoon period.**” During this phase, Amazon tests your product to see whether customers like it. If you play your [SEO and PPC](https://www.bellavix.com/amazon-ppc-for-beginners-a-simple-strategy-to-launch-and-scale-new-products/) cards right during the first 90 days, you can build momentum fast instead of becoming another forgotten listing buried on page 17.

This guide breaks down exactly what to do during your first 90 days on Amazon in 2026. No fluff. No fake guru nonsense. Just a clean launch roadmap.

#### Key Goals for Your First 90 Days

*   Build keyword relevance
*   Generate early conversions
*   Collect reviews ethically
*   Improve click-through rate
*   Train Amazon’s algorithm
*   Lower ACOS over time
*   Create ranking momentum
*   Scale winning keywords

Common Mistake

Why It Damages Your Amazon Growth

Launching Without Keyword Research

Amazon struggles to understand your product relevance, which hurts indexing and rankings.

Turning Off PPC Too Early

You lose keyword momentum and organic ranking signals before your product gains traction.

Keyword Stuffing

Overloading keywords makes listings look spammy and reduces conversion rates.

Ignoring Click-Through Rate (CTR)

Even with traffic, poor CTR tells Amazon shoppers are not interested in your listing.

Using Low-Quality Images

Weak visuals reduce trust instantly and lower conversion potential.

Changing Listings Too Frequently

Constant edits confuse Amazon’s algorithm and disrupt ranking stability.

No Review Strategy

Products without reviews struggle to build trust and convert visitors into buyers.

Overspending on Broad Keywords

Generic keywords burn budget fast without delivering profitable conversions.

 

### Recommended Campaign Structure

*   1 Auto Campaign
*   1 Broad Match Campaign
*   1 Phrase Match Campaign
*   1 Exact Match Campaign
*   1 Product Targeting Campaign

#### Final Thoughts

[Amazon in 2026](https://ecomgliders.com/services/#service_5) is brutally competitive. That’s just reality. But the sellers who win are usually not the smartest people in the room. They’re the most consistent.

Your first 90 days matter more than most people realize. Every click, conversion, keyword, and PPC adjustment teaches Amazon something about your product.

Treat the launch phase like planting seeds. Water them daily. Don’t dig them up every morning to see if they’re growing. That’s how new sellers destroy momentum before it even starts.

Play the long game. The algorithm notices.
