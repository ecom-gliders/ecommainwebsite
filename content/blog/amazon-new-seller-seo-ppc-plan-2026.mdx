## What should a new Amazon seller do in the first 90 days?

In the first 90 days, a new Amazon seller should optimize the listing for indexing and conversion, run structured PPC to generate early sales data, harvest search terms into exact coverage and negatives, collect ethical reviews, and scale only profitable keywords. Early consistency matters more than constant listing rewrites.

Amazon is crowded. New ASINs need relevance signals and sales activity before organic coverage becomes reliable. Some sellers describe an early testing window when Amazon evaluates customer response. Treat that as a reason to launch with a clear plan, not as a guarantee of easy rank.

This guide outlines a clean 90-day SEO and PPC roadmap for 2026. ## Key goals for the first 90 days

*   Build keyword relevance and indexing
*   Generate early conversions
*   Collect reviews through Amazon-compliant methods
*   Improve click-through rate
*   Lower ACOS over time as data improves
*   Create ranking momentum
*   Scale winning keywords carefully

## 90-day timeline

| Timeline | Main focus | SEO actions | PPC actions | Expected result |
|----------|------------|-------------|-------------|-----------------|
| Days 1-30 | Launch and indexing | Optimize title, bullets, backend keywords, images, and A+ Content | Start Auto plus controlled Broad discovery | Indexing and initial traffic |
| Days 31-60 | Ranking and cleanup | Double down on converting keywords; improve CTR | Move winners into Phrase and Exact | Better rank signals; lower waste |
| Days 61-90 | Scaling momentum | Add long-tail coverage from search terms | Raise bids on profitable keywords; scale carefully | More stable organic + sales growth |

## Common mistakes that slow new sellers

| Mistake | Why it hurts |
|---------|----------------|
| Launching without keyword research | Weak relevance and indexing |
| Turning off PPC too early | Lose sales and keyword learning before traction |
| Keyword stuffing | Hurts readability and conversion |
| Ignoring CTR | Signals weak search-result appeal |
| Low-quality images | Reduces trust and conversion |
| Changing listings constantly | Disrupts learning and ranking stability |
| No review plan | Harder to convert cold traffic |
| Overspending on broad head terms | Burns budget without profit |

## Recommended campaign structure

Keep structure simple enough to read:

*   1 Auto campaign
*   1 Broad match discovery campaign
*   1 Phrase match campaign
*   1 Exact match campaign
*   1 Product targeting campaign

Harvest search terms weekly (or more often on high spend). Move winners; negate losers. Structure detail: [Amazon PPC campaign structure](/amazon-ppc-campaign-structure/).

## Bottom line

The first 90 days teach Amazon what your product is and whether shoppers buy it. Consistency beats constant rewrites. Optimize the listing once with care, run disciplined PPC, and let search-term data guide SEO and bid decisions.
