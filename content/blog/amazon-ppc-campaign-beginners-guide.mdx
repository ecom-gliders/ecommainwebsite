## What Amazon PPC is

Amazon PPC is pay-per-click advertising on Amazon: you bid on keywords or products, ads show in search or detail placements, and you pay for clicks. Soft beginner formats:

| Soft type | Soft beginner fit |
|-----------|-------------------|
| Sponsored Products | Best first campaign for most sellers |
| Sponsored Brands | After Brand Registry / multi-ASIN readiness |
| Sponsored Display | Optional retargeting later |

## Soft prep before spend

*   Optimize title, images, bullets, and price so clicks can convert  
*   Soft keyword research via Amazon search suggestions and competitor scans—tool brands are optional, not required  
*   Soft claim: “start with $20–$30/day” is a soft learning budget example, not a verified rule—**Requires confirmation** for your margin  
*   Soft practice: know contribution margin before raising bids  

## Soft first-campaign steps (**Proposed**)

1. Seller Central → Advertising → Campaign Manager → Create campaign  
2. Choose Sponsored Products  
3. Name campaign; set daily budget and end date (or ongoing)  
4. Add the ASIN(s)  
5. Start with automatic targeting for discovery  
6. Soft bidding: dynamic down-only while learning—**Assumed**  
7. Launch; review search-term reports before scaling  

## Soft auto vs manual

*   **Auto:** Amazon matches queries; good for learning search terms  
*   **Manual:** you pick broad/phrase/exact; better control after you have data  
*   Soft practice: harvest converting terms from auto into manual; add negatives for waste  

## Soft weekly beginner hygiene

*   Pause or negative irrelevant search terms  
*   Raise only terms that clear your margin target  
*   Soft metrics: ACoS, TACOS, CTR, CVR—together, not ACoS alone  
*   Soft claim: case stories that cut ACoS from 60% to 28% or grew sales 40% are unverified here—**Not yet tested**

## Soft mistakes to avoid

*   Ads on a weak listing  
*   Spend without reading reports  
*   No negatives  
*   Auto-only forever  
*   No clear objective (learning vs profitability)  

## Soft next steps after basics

*   Separate match types for cleaner data  
*   Cautious placement modifiers after enough data  
*   Soft dayparting only when hourly patterns are clear—**Requires confirmation**  
*   Soft claim: third-party PPC tools are optional; not endorsements  

## Bottom line

Soft claim: beginner Amazon PPC success is listing quality plus search-term hygiene, not a one-click launch. Soft claim: treat “complete guide / grow sales” marketing as unverified; start small, measure, then scale.
