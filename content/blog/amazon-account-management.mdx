## What seller account management covers

| Soft workstream | Soft purpose |
|-----------------|--------------|
| Listing quality | Compliance, conversion, contribution conflicts |
| Inventory / FBA | Stockouts, overstock, inbound accuracy |
| Advertising | PPC structure, TACOS, waste control |
| Account health | Metrics, policy notices, case hygiene |
| Customer messaging | Response SLAs, complaint themes |
| Reporting | Weekly decisions, not vanity dashboards |

Soft claim: buyer-account and subscription management are different intents from marketplace ops—**Assumed**.

## Soft why it matters

Without a cadence, sellers often see rising ad spend with flat conversion, repeating stock issues, suppressed listings, or account-health friction. Soft claim: structured management saves owner time and improves decision quality; it does not automatically boost revenue—**Assumed**.

## Soft seller operating cadence (**Proposed**)

**Daily**

*   Critical orders, account-health alerts, broken campaigns  
*   Urgent customer messages  

**Weekly**

*   Inventory cover vs lead time  
*   Search-term hygiene and bid adjustments  
*   Listing suppressions / contribution drift  

**Monthly**

*   Fee and margin review  
*   Catalog and creative refresh priorities  
*   Soft security hygiene for Seller Central users (permissions, 2FA)—**Assumed**
