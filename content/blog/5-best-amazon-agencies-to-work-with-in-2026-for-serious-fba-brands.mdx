Serious FBA brands usually need an Amazon partner that combines PPC discipline, listing and catalog work, and clear accountability. This guide compares five options with different strengths: a dedicated Amazon growth agency, a senior-only full-service shop, an AI PPC platform, a high-volume full-service consultant, and a software-empowered retail media agency.

## Why Amazon agencies matter in 2026

If you run a serious Amazon FBA brand in 2026, choosing the right agency is a major growth decision. Advertising costs are rising, competition is intense across many categories, and durable brands usually combine strong products with expert marketplace execution.

Amazon PPC goes beyond launching a few auto campaigns. It needs structured campaign architecture across Sponsored Products, Sponsored Brands, Sponsored Display, and sometimes DSP, aligned with ranking and profitability. Brands also need listing optimization, review and rating support, account health monitoring, and timely responses to competitors and policy changes.

For many growing brands, the right Amazon agency is the fastest way to add proven systems and operator-level decision-making without building a large in-house team.

### How these five agencies were selected

We focused on agencies that meet four criteria in 2026:

*   Clear Amazon specialization or a dedicated Amazon division (not "we also do Amazon")
*   Documented experience, case studies, or retention signals that support longer-term results
*   Strong processes and/or technology behind PPC management
*   Transparent positioning: you can understand what they do, for whom, and how

Within that framework, here are five Amazon agencies to evaluate in 2026, and the problems each is built to solve.

### Key questions to ask before you hire

*   How do you define good and bad ACOS or ROAS for my category?
*   How often do you optimize campaigns, and what exactly do you change?
*   What is your strategy for scaling a campaign that is already performing well?
*   What do you do if performance drops suddenly?
*   Who will manage my account, and how many accounts do they handle?

Answers to these questions show whether a partner can solve your specific problems, not only speak about growth in general.
