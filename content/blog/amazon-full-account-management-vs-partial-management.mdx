## Amazon full account management vs partial management: what is the difference?

Full account management means one team owns most Seller Central operations—listings, ads, inventory coordination, health hygiene, and reporting—under shared KPIs. Partial management means you buy one or two slices (often PPC-only or listing-only) while strategy, inventory, and other work stay with you or another vendor. The choice is about accountability and integration, not which model “wins Amazon.”

## What full account management covers

Typical end-to-end scope:

*   Account health monitoring and case support (not a suspension guarantee)
*   Listing architecture, A+ where eligible, and image sequencing
*   Sponsored ads and TACoS discipline; DSP only when contracted
*   Inventory and inbound coordination with your supply plan
*   Brand protection workflows when Brand Registry and tools are in place

Advantage: one source of truth across ads, catalog, and operations. Soft claim: fewer handoff gaps. Tradeoff: higher retainer and less day-to-day control unless approvals are clear.

## What partial management covers

Common partial scopes:

*   PPC management only
*   Listing or content optimization only
*   Account health or appeal support
*   Launch or creative packages

Partial works when you have strong internal strategy, inventory ownership, and a clear owner who stitches vendors together. Limitations: fragmented accountability, ads/listings misalignment, and more seller coordination time. Soft claim: lower fees do not always mean lower total cost if waste elsewhere rises.

## Comparison

| Factor | Full management | Partial management |
|--------|-----------------|--------------------|
| Scope | Most ops under one team | One or few workstreams |
| Control | Approvals + reporting | You keep strategy and glue work |
| Cost shape | Higher retainer | Lower line item; more internal time |
| Best when | Complex catalog, limited founder time | Strong in-house team; testing a specialty |
| Risk | Vendor dependency | Gaps between vendors |

Neither model guarantees Buy Box share, higher LTV, or policy safety. Soft outcomes depend on execution quality and category.

## Which to choose

Choose full management when you want fewer vendors and unified reporting. Choose partial when you need specialist execution (for example PPC) while keeping inventory and brand decisions in-house. Start partial and expand only if coordination cost exceeds the retainer difference.
