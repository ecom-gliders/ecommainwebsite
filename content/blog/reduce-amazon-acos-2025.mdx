# How to Reduce Amazon ACoS in 2025: 7 Data-Backed Hacks That Cut Ad Spend by 34%

## How to Reduce Amazon ACoS in 2025: 7 Data-Backed Hacks

**How to Reduce Amazon ACoS in 2025: 7 Data-Backed Hacks That Cut Ad Spend by 34%**

*Word-count ≈ 1 500 | Reading time ≈ 5 min*

**Quick Answer**

Amazon ACoS drops fastest when you **pause bleeding keywords, harvest winners daily, and shift 60 % of budget to Sponsored Brands video**. Our clients cut ACoS from 34 % → 21 % in 30 days using the playbook below.

#### 1\. Bleeding-Keyword Amputation (Do Today)

Open **Campaign Manager** **▸ Advertising Reports** **▸ Search Term Report**  
Filter: **Orders = 0 & Spend ≥ $10** → add as **exact negative keywords**.  
Example: “iphone 13 case cheap” burned $43 with zero sales — gone in 60 seconds.

#### 2\. Auto → Manual Goldmine (Days 2-5)

*   **Download** last 30-day search term report.
*   **Filter:** Orders ≥ 2 + CTR ≥ 0.3 %.
*   **Create new manual exact campaign** with bids 15 % lower than auto.
*   **Move budget** from auto; leave auto on discovery only.

Kitchen brand moved 47 terms → ACoS fell 8 % overnight.

#### 3\. Bid-by-Placement Rules (Day 6)

*   Campaign ▸ Settings ▸ **Adjust Bids by Placement**  
    • **Top of Search +30 %** (converts 2×)  
    • **Rest of Search -25 %** (bleeds clicks)
    
    Same revenue, **19 % less spend**.
    

![How to Reduce Amazon ACoS in 2025 7 Data-Backed Hacks](https://ecomgliders.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/08/How-to-Reduce-Amazon-ACoS-in-2025-7-Data-Backed-Hacks.png)

#### 4\. Dayparting with Amazon Marketing Stream

Use **Amazon Marketing Stream** to pause ads 02:00-07:00 local time (low CR).  
No code? Use **Perpetua’s dayparting slider** .

#### 5\. Sponsored Brands Video Hijack

Amazon Q1-2025 report: **SBV delivers 35 % lower ACoS** than Sponsored Products .  
Template: 15-sec vertical UGC, product in use, bold text “#1 Seller”.  
Budget Shift: Move 60 % SP budget → SBV.  
Client Win: $8 K spend → $2.7 K saved in 14 days.

#### 6\. External Traffic Boost (TikTok Spark Ads)

1.  Create **TikTok Spark Ad** with **Amazon Attribution tag** .
2.  Target #amazonfinds, $20/day.
3.  **BSR jump 12 %** → CPC drops → ACoS falls.

#### 7\. Weekly Bid-Decay Formula

Formula: New Bid = Current × (Target ACoS ÷ Current ACoS)  
Run every Monday. Automate inside **Helium 10 Adtomic** or **Kenji ROI PPC Growth**.
