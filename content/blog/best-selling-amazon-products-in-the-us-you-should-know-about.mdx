For Amazon sellers, knowing which products lead U.S. demand helps with sourcing, listing focus, and inventory planning. In 2025, trending items range from viral drinkware to eco-friendly sheets and tech gadgets. Here is a look at strong performers and what they signal for store growth.

![Best-Selling Amazon Products in the U.S. You Should Know About](/images/blog/Best-Selling-Amazon-Products-in-the-U.S.-You-Should-Know-About-55342428.png)

### What’s Trending on Amazon Right Now

#### Cooling Sheets & Bedding

With intense heat waves across the U.S., cooling sheet sets under $25—like those from Bedsure and Amazon Basics—are selling quickly. According to Southern Living’s guide to cooling sheets, these lightweight, moisture-wicking options are generating thousands of five-star reviews.

#### Portable Carpet Cleaners

The **BISSELL Little Green Max** has surged in sales thanks to its compact design and 30% stronger suction, making it a favorite during Prime Day events.

#### Summer Tank Tops

The **Amazon Essentials Slim-Fit Tank Top** features over 50,000 five-star reviews and under-$10 pricing. Its versatile design makes it a summer wardrobe staple for shoppers.

#### Budget Tablets with Premium Features

The **[Amazon Fire HD 8 Plus](https://www.amazon.com/Fire-HD-8-Plus/dp/B0LBL6JF2G)** is currently over 50% off. With 3 GB RAM, wireless charging, and Show Mode, this tablet delivers strong value and remains a leader in the budget tablet market.

#### Color-Changing Water Bottles

Pyrex’s **color-changing water bottles**—UV-reactive and shatterproof—are attracting attention, offering an affordable and unique alternative to Stanley and Yeti at around $16.

### Evergreen Best-Sellers That Stay Strong

```checklist
- **Amazon Basics Printer Paper** consistently earns over $40 million in monthly sales—making it one of the most reliable staples to sell.
- **Stanley Quencher H2.0 Tumblers**, especially the 40 oz version, have seen a 275% year-over-year sales increase, thanks to social media buzz.
- **Apple’s 2025 lineup**—the MacBook Air, iPad 10th Gen, and Apple Watch Series 10—remain top seller picks among tech-savvy buyers.
- **Essential consumables** like Bounty, Charmin, and Premier Protein shakes continue to top the top-100 list due to their repeat-purchase nature.
```

These categories show why demand stays high: clear use cases, strong reviews, and either seasonal spikes or repeat purchase cycles. Sellers evaluating new ASINs can use the same signals—review volume, price band, and replenishment behavior—to prioritize opportunities.
