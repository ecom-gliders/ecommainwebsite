### What’s Trending on Amazon Right Now

#### Cooling Sheets & Bedding

With intense heat waves across the U.S., cooling sheet sets under $25—like those from Bedsure and Amazon Basics—are flying off the virtual shelves. According to Southern Living’s guide to cooling sheets, these lightweight, moisture-wicking options are generating thousands of five-star reviews.

#### Portable Carpet Cleaners

The **BISSELL Little Green Max** has surged in sales thanks to its compact design and 30% stronger suction, making it a favorite during Prime Day events.

#### Summer Tank Tops

The **Amazon Essentials Slim-Fit Tank Top** features over 50,000 five-star reviews and under-$10 pricing. Its versatile design makes it a summer wardrobe staple for shoppers.

#### Budget Tablets with Premium Features

The **[Amazon Fire HD 8 Plus](https://www.amazon.com/Fire-HD-8-Plus/dp/B0LBL6JF2G)** is currently over 50% off. With 3 GB RAM, wireless charging, and Show Mode, this tablet delivers strong value and continues to dominate the budget tablet market.

#### Color‑Changing Water Bottles

Pyrex’s innovative **color-changing water bottles**—UV-reactive and shatterproof—are attracting attention, offering an affordable and unique alternative to Stanley and Yeti at around $16.
