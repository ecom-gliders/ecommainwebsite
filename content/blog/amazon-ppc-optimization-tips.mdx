3. Soft competitive scan: placements and creative patterns as ideas, not copy  

## Soft optimization practices

### Soft keywords and match types

*   Broad/auto: discovery  
*   Phrase: mid control  
*   Exact: scale proven terms  
*   Soft twin → [Amazon PPC keyword research](/amazon-ppc-strategy-keyword-research-2025/)

### Soft negatives

*   Block irrelevant intents from search-term reports  
*   Soft claim: negatives can cut waste; they do not “significantly” guarantee ACoS drops—**Assumed**

### Soft bids and placements

*   Soften bids on weak converters after enough clicks  
*   Raise carefully on proven terms  
*   Soft Top of Search vs Product Pages multipliers—test; outcomes **Require confirmation**

### Soft structure

*   Separate by product, match type, or stage where volume justifies it  
*   Soft branded vs non-branded split when branded volume is material—**Assumed**

## Soft ACoS hygiene (not a guarantee)

*   Prefer terms with acceptable conversion after sample size  
*   Pause or cut chronic waste  
*   Align listing title/bullets/images with ad queries
