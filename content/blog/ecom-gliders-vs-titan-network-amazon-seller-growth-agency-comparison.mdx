## How do eCom Gliders and Titan Network compare?

eCom Gliders is a full-service Amazon seller growth agency that executes PPC, listing optimization, creative, catalog, and account management for brands. Titan Network publicly positions as an ecommerce operator network and Amazon mastermind with coaching programs, playbooks, tools, and peer accountability where members implement the work (Observed on titannetwork.com / discover.titannetwork.com, 2026-10-30). They are not the same category. One is done-for-you agency execution; the other is education and operator community support.

An older draft on this URL incorrectly treated Titan Network as a peer full-service agency and framed eCom Gliders as only "basic tactical" support. That framing was inaccurate. Separately, Titan-related done-for-you PPC offers may appear under other Titan product brands (for example Titan Ignite public PPC pages). Treat those as distinct from Titan Network membership unless a proposal says otherwise. Requires confirmation per offer.

## What Titan Network publicly offers

Based on Titan Network public pages (Observed 2026-10-30), the network markets:

*   Tiered programs (for example Genesis, Growth, Scale, Elite naming on public pages)
*   Coaching, playbooks, and operator community / mastermind structure
*   Amazon-focused education covering PPC, product, and scaling systems
*   Proprietary tools and SOP-style resources for members
*   A model where sellers remain operators and implement guidance

That can be valuable for founders who want to build internal capability. It is not automatically a substitute for a managed PPC and listing team unless you also buy a separate done-for-you service.

## Which model fits which brand?

**Consider eCom Gliders if you:**

*   Need specialists to run campaigns and improve listings for you
*   Want ads, creative, and catalog work coordinated under one Amazon agency
*   Prefer audit-led prioritization before scaling spend
*   Do not have bandwidth to implement a full operator curriculum yourself

**Consider evaluating Titan Network if you:**

*   Want coaching, frameworks, and peer accountability
*   Prefer to keep execution in-house
*   Are building operator skills across product, PPC, and systems
*   Accept that results depend on your team's follow-through

Some brands use education networks and agencies at different stages. The mismatch to avoid is paying for coaching when you actually needed execution—or hiring an agency when you wanted to learn to operate everything yourself.
