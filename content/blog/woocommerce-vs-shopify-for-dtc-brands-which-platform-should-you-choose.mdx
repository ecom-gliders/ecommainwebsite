### Introduction

Building a successful DTC (Direct‑to‑Consumer) store means choosing the right ecommerce platform—your foundation for growth and brand identity. When comparing **WooCommerce vs Shopify for DTC**, WooCommerce offers unmatched flexibility and control, while Shopify brings speed, simplicity, and ease-of-use to the table. In this guide, tailored for Amazon sellers, new DTC brand owners, and growing ecommerce businesses, we’ll explore which platform best fits your needs in 2025.

![WooCommerce vs Shopify for DTC Brands Which Platform Should You Choose](/images/blog/WooCommerce-vs-Shopify-for-DTC-Brands-Which-Platform-Should-You-Choose-1024x683-085ecd49.png)

### Overview

| Feature | WooCommerce | Shopify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting | Self‑hosted on WordPress | Fully hosted, secure, and maintained |
| Cost | Free core + hosting & extensions | $39–$399/mo, plus subscription fees |
| Ease of Setup | 1–3 days, technical setup | Under a day with intuitive onboarding |
| Customization | Full code access, unlimited plugins | Pre-built themes, limited code tweaks |
| SEO & Content | WordPress SEO power, Yoast support | Basic SEO — limited customization |
| Apps & Extensions | 800+ Woo extensions | 8,000+ quality‑controlled apps |
| Support | Community forums; no 24/7 support | 24/7 support, documentation |
| Scaling | Dependent on hosting & optimization | Stable scaling, handles spikes |
| Market Share | ~20% of ecommerce sites | ~26% market share, high traffic preference |

#### 1. Pricing & Total Cost of Ownership

**WooCommerce** has no core fees—it’s a free WordPress plugin. But you’ll pay for:

```checklist
- Hosting (~$120/year)
- SSL certificates & domain (~$15/year)
- Premium themes and essential extensions → Monthly cost ranges from $4–$480 depending on scale
```

**Shopify** ranges from **$39–$399/month**, with higher tiers offering lower transaction fees and advanced features .

```tip
TITLE: Tip
For predictable budgeting, Shopify’s clearer pricing wins. But for low-volume stores or those prioritizing customization, Woo Commerce’s flexibility often leads to lower overall cost.
```
