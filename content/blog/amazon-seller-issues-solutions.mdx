## Soft issue map

| Issue area | Soft first response |
|------------|---------------------|
| Identity verification stuck | Match legal name exactly; clear color scans; submit once; wait before re-upload |
| Seller Central login / OTP | Reset credentials; authenticator over SMS; try another browser; confirm no hold |
| Seller Central outage / glitch | Clear cache; avoid bulk edits mid-outage; check Amazon status communications |
| Suppressed listings | Read Suppressed tab reason; fix attributes/images/policy flags |
| Contribution not updating | CLR bulk edit where appropriate; Brand Registry where enrolled; case with evidence |
| Buy Box loss | Review price, metrics, stock, fulfillment type—not price alone |
| Complaints / ODR pressure | Reply fast; resolve root cause; track repeats |
| Account Health flags | Daily check; prioritize red/yellow; keep authenticity invoices |

## Soft identity verification

Soft claim: mismatched names, blurry scans, unsupported docs, and repeat uploads without waiting often delay activation—**Assumed**.

Soft practice (**Proposed**):

*   Government ID + recent address proof + bank/card statement when requested  
*   Exact name match to Seller Central  
*   Color, complete scans; avoid glare and crops  
*   One clean submission; wait before re-uploading  
*   Escalate with Seller Support if repeated rejection after correct docs  

Soft claim: passport vs license preference and translation rules vary by region—**Requires confirmation**. Soft claim: branded email is optional professionalism, not a verified approval factor—**Assumed**.

## Soft Seller Central access issues

*   Login rejects: disable autofill; try Chrome/Firefox; confirm password  
*   OTP failures: prefer authenticator; confirm phone/email; check spam  
*   Dashboard stuck: clear cookies; disable extensions; try private window  
*   Soft practice: user permissions for trusted staff so one lockout does not stop ops  
*   Soft claim: VPN/public Wi-Fi can trigger security friction—**Assumed**

## Soft listing suppression and contributions

Soft claim: incomplete attributes, policy flags, non-compliant images, and multi-seller contribution clashes commonly suppress visibility—**Assumed**.

Soft fix path (**Proposed**):

1. Manage Inventory → Suppressed tab → read Amazon’s reason  
2. Fix images (size/background/compliance), required attributes, or pricing flags as stated  
3. Recheck after Amazon’s refresh window  
4. Open a case with screenshots if still suppressed  

Related depth: [Amazon suppressed listings fix](/amazon-suppressed-listings-fix/), [branded listing contributions guide](/amazon-branded-listing-contributions-guide/).

## Soft Buy Box and pricing

Soft claim: Buy Box eligibility depends on price competitiveness, seller metrics, stock continuity, and fulfillment type—among other signals—**Assumed**. Soft claim: “80% of purchases go through the Buy Box” is widely repeated online and is **Not yet tested** here—do not treat as verified.

Soft practice:

*   Review competitor pricing without racing below contribution margin  
*   Watch ODR, late shipment, and tracking validity  
*   Keep safety stock to reduce OOS Buy Box loss—**Assumed**  
*   Soft claim: FBA often helps eligibility vs FBM for many ASINs; not guaranteed—**Assumed**  

Soft claim: metric thresholds below are commonly cited; confirm current Seller Central targets for your store—**Requires confirmation**:

*   ODR often discussed under ~1%  
*   Late shipment often discussed under ~4%  
*   Valid tracking often discussed above ~95%  
*   Pre-fulfillment cancel often discussed under ~2.5%  

## Soft complaints and account health

*   Monitor Account Health daily  
*   Acknowledge buyer issues quickly; refund/replace when appropriate  
*   Fix packaging or listing root causes when complaints repeat  
*   Soft sections to watch: customer service performance, policy compliance, product policy compliance

2. Weekly: contribution conflicts, Buy Box share review, complaint themes  
3. Monthly: metric trends, invoice readiness, policy training for staff
