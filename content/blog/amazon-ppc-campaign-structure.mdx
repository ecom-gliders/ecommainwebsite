## How should you structure Amazon PPC campaigns in 2026?

Structure Amazon PPC campaigns in three layers: portfolios for budget grouping, campaigns for type and bidding rules, and ad groups for products plus keyword themes. Keep auto and manual targeting separate, keep themes tight, and harvest search terms into controlled match types. Weak structure hides winners and lets waste scale with the budget.

If ads spend without consistent return, the issue is often organization, not only bids or keyword lists. A messy setup makes it hard to see what works, control spend, or scale calmly.

## Why campaign structure matters

Keyword research and bidding only perform as well as the structure underneath them. A clear structure gives you:

*   Clearer performance data by keyword, match type, and product
*   Better budget control so one term cannot silently drain a shared cap
*   Faster optimization decisions
*   Cleaner scaling when you add ASINs or audiences

Without structure, reports stay noisy. With it, optimization compounds.

## The three-level hierarchy

Amazon advertising is organized as:

*   **Portfolio** - Groups related campaigns; optional shared budget caps
*   **Campaign** - Sets type, daily budget, targeting method, and bidding strategy
*   **Ad group** - Holds product ads and the keywords or product targets assigned to them

Bid changes, keyword harvesting, and negatives happen at these levels. Knowing which level to change prevents constant rebuilds.

## Step 1: Choose campaign types deliberately

Start with Sponsored Products. Add Sponsored Brands and Sponsored Display when you have enough converting data.

*   **Sponsored Products** - Keyword and product targeting in search and on detail pages; clearest spend-to-sales path for most sellers
*   **Sponsored Brands** - Brand creative and multi-ASIN placements; useful after SP proves top converting queries
*   **Sponsored Display** - Audience and product-page reach on and off Amazon

## Step 2: Separate auto and manual campaigns

Do not mix automatic and manual targeting in the same campaign or ad group.

*   **Auto** - Amazon places ads from listing signals. Useful for discovery, especially on launches, with limited control
*   **Manual** - You choose keywords or ASINs and control bids more tightly

Run them separately. Use auto as a research feed: mine search term reports and move strong terms into manual campaigns with controlled bids and negatives.

## Step 3: Tighten ad group setup

Ad groups are where structure becomes granular.

*   Prefer one ASIN (or tightly related variation set) per ad group when volumes allow, so bids match margin and conversion reality
*   Keep each ad group to a tight keyword theme. Example: do not mix "yoga mat," "exercise mat," and "gym floor mat" themes in one ad group if they behave differently

Optional: for a small set of proven exact keywords, dedicated single-keyword ad groups can clarify data. Use selectively; over-splitting creates management noise.

Match-type hygiene: avoid the same keyword competing against itself across match types inside one ad group.

## Common structural mistakes

*   Too many unrelated keywords in one ad group
*   Same keyword in multiple match types within one ad group
*   Auto and manual sharing one uncontrolled budget pool without visibility
*   No negative keyword process
*   One campaign for every product with no ASIN-level readability

## Bottom line

A strong Amazon PPC campaign structure in 2026 is a data and control system: separate auto and manual, organize by ASIN and theme, use match types with intent, apply negatives, and use portfolios as accounts grow.
